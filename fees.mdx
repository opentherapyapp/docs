**Clients pay the therapist's published price and nothing more.** No booking fee, no service charge, no surcharge.

**Open Therapy takes no percentage of a one-to-one session.** The introduction fee is charged once, the first time a client books with a therapist, and never again for that pair.

## What a client pays

The price shown on the profile.

Therapy is GST-free health care in Australia, so a client's session invoice carries no GST. See [Paying for sessions](/clients/paying-for-sessions).

## What a therapist pays

Listing is free — no monthly fee, no seat licence, no minimum. Three things come off a session, and only when there is one.

| Deduction | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Listing | **\$0** | Forever |
| Introduction fee | **\$70–\$120**, by discipline | Once per new client, ever |
| Card processing | Stripe's actual cost | Every paid session |
| GST | **10%** on the introduction fee | With that fee |

<Note>
  Card processing is passed through at cost, quoted at Stripe's published Australian domestic rate of
  **1.7% + 30c**. What's deducted is whatever Stripe actually charged for that card.
</Note>

### The introduction fee

Charged the first time a particular client books with a particular therapist, and never again for that pair. A client who sees two therapists generates two introduction fees.

Fees are quoted ex-GST, with 10% added on top.

| Discipline | Introduction fee |
|---|---|
| Psychiatrist | \$120.00 |
| Psychologist | \$90.00 |
| Clinical Psychologist | \$90.00 |
| Counsellor | \$70.00 |
| Speech Pathologist | \$70.00 |
| Occupational Therapist | \$70.00 |
| Physiotherapist | \$70.00 |
| Any other discipline | \$90.00 |

Two cases where it isn't charged at all:

## A worked example

A psychologist charging **\$180** for a 50-minute session, paid by an Australian card.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="First session with a new client">
    | Line | Amount |
    |---|---|
    | Client pays | \$180.00 |
    | Introduction fee (Psychologist) | −\$90.00 |
    | GST on the introduction fee | −\$9.00 |
    | Card processing | −\$3.36 |
    | **You receive** | **\$77.64** |

    Total deducted: \$102.36, or 56.9%.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Every session after that">
    | Line | Amount |
    |---|---|
    | Client pays | \$180.00 |
    | Introduction fee | \$0.00 |
    | Card processing | −\$3.36 |
    | **You receive** | **\$176.64** |

    Total deducted: \$3.36, or 1.9% — card processing and nothing else.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The rate you actually pay depends on how long clients stay:

| | |
|---|---|
| Six sessions with one new client | \$1,080.00 collected, \$119.16 deducted — **11.0%** |
| Twelve sessions with one new client | \$2,160.00 collected, \$139.32 deducted — **6.5%** |

## Group programs are the one place a percentage applies

A group enrolment carries a flat **15%** commission plus GST. There is no introduction fee on a group place, and card processing is **not** passed through — Open Therapy absorbs Stripe's cost on group enrolments.

One place on a course priced at \$240.00:

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Client pays | \$240.00 |
| Commission (15%) | −\$36.00 |
| GST on the commission | −\$3.60 |
| Card processing | \$0.00 |
| **You receive** | **\$200.40** |

See [Running a group program](/therapists/groups).

## GST

GST is shown as its own line rather than folded into a percentage.

- **The session** is GST-free.
- **Open Therapy's fees** are quoted ex-GST with 10% added on top.
- **Card processing** arrives GST-inclusive. Its tax is carved back out for the invoice rather than added again.

## Fee changes never re-price the past

Every settlement stores the rates charged at the time it was made. A rate change applies to new sessions only.

The retired **platform fee** line survives for that reason. It was a percentage of every session, and the rate is now zero. It appears only on statements old enough to have paid one, and is never charged on a new session.
