Two rules sit above everything on this page.

**Clients pay the therapist's published price and nothing more.** No booking fee, no service charge, no surcharge.

**Open Therapy takes no percentage of a one-to-one session.** There is no commission on the hour. The introduction fee is charged once, the first time a client books with a therapist, and never again for that pair.

## What a client pays

The price shown on the profile. That is the whole answer.

Therapy is GST-free health care in Australia, so a client's session invoice carries no GST. See [Paying for sessions](/clients/paying-for-sessions).

## What a therapist pays

Listing is free — no monthly fee, no seat licence, no minimum. Three things come off a session, and only when there is a session.

| Deduction | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Listing | **\$0** | Forever |
| Introduction fee | **\$70–\$120**, by discipline | Once per new client, ever |
| Card processing | Stripe's actual cost | Every paid session |
| GST | **10%** on the introduction fee | With that fee |

<Note>
  Card processing is passed through at cost, never marked up. Before a payment exists it's quoted
  using Stripe's published Australian domestic rate of **1.7% + 30c**, but what's deducted is
  whatever Stripe actually charged for that particular card, read from the transaction.
</Note>

### The introduction fee

A one-off that pays for the match rather than the hour. It's charged the first time a particular client books with a particular therapist, and never again for that pair — not on the second session, not on the two hundredth. A client who sees two therapists generates two introduction fees, one for each.

Fees are quoted ex-GST, with 10% added on top.

| Discipline | Introduction fee |
|---|---|
| Psychiatrist | \$120.00 |
| Psychologist | \$90.00 |
| Clinical Psychologist | \$90.00 |
| Counsellor | \$70.00 |
| Speech Pathologist | \$70.00 |
| Occupational Therapist | \$70.00 |
| Physiotherapist | \$70.00 |
| Any other discipline | \$90.00 |

Two cases where it isn't charged at all:

## A worked example

A psychologist charging **\$180** for a 50-minute session, paid by an Australian card.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="First session with a new client">
    | Line | Amount |
    |---|---|
    | Client pays | \$180.00 |
    | Introduction fee (Psychologist) | −\$90.00 |
    | GST on the introduction fee | −\$9.00 |
    | Card processing | −\$3.36 |
    | **You receive** | **\$77.64** |

    Total deducted: \$102.36, or 56.9% — almost all of it the one-off introduction.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Every session after that">
    | Line | Amount |
    |---|---|
    | Client pays | \$180.00 |
    | Introduction fee | \$0.00 |
    | Card processing | −\$3.36 |
    | **You receive** | **\$176.64** |

    Total deducted: \$3.36, or 1.9% — card processing and nothing else.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The first session is where Open Therapy earns and every one after it is close to cost, so the rate you actually pay depends on how long clients stay rather than on any published percentage:

| | |
|---|---|
| Six sessions with one new client | \$1,080.00 collected, \$119.16 deducted — **11.0%** |
| Twelve sessions with one new client | \$2,160.00 collected, \$139.32 deducted — **6.5%** |

## Group programs are the one place a percentage applies

A group enrolment carries a flat **15%** commission plus GST. There is no introduction fee on a group place, and card processing is **not** passed through — Open Therapy absorbs Stripe's cost on group enrolments.

One place on a course priced at \$240.00:

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Client pays | \$240.00 |
| Commission (15%) | −\$36.00 |
| GST on the commission | −\$3.60 |
| Card processing | \$0.00 |
| **You receive** | **\$200.40** |

See [Running a group program](/therapists/groups).

## GST

GST is shown as its own line rather than folded into a percentage, so your invoice reflects what was actually remitted.

- **The session** is GST-free. Health services of this kind aren't taxable.
- **Open Therapy's fees** are quoted ex-GST with 10% added on top.
- **Card processing** arrives GST-inclusive, because Australian card fees are published that way. Its tax is carved back out for the invoice rather than added again.

## Fee changes never re-price the past

Every settlement stores the rates charged at the time it was made. If a rate changes tomorrow it applies to new sessions only — it cannot re-price what you earned yesterday.

That is also why a **platform fee** line still exists in the product. It was a percentage of every session and it has been retired: the rate is zero. It appears on a statement only if that statement is old enough to have paid one, and it is never charged on a new session.
