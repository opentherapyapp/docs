**You keep the published session price. Card processing is added at checkout.** There is no booking fee and no percentage of a one-to-one session.

**Open Therapy takes no percentage of a one-to-one session.** The introduction fee is charged once, the first time a client books with a therapist, and never again for that pair.

## What a client pays

The price shown on the profile, plus **card processing** — Stripe's published Australian domestic rate of **1.7% + 30c**, quoted so it covers the total charged, not only the session. On a \$180 session that is **\$3.42**, so the card is charged **\$183.42**. On a \$220 session it is **\$4.11**, so **\$224.11**.

There is no booking fee and no service charge on top of that. Therapy is GST-free health care in Australia, so a client's session invoice carries no GST. See [Paying for sessions](/clients/paying-for-sessions).

## What a therapist pays

Listing is free — no monthly fee, no seat licence, no minimum. You keep the published session price. The introduction fee (and GST on it) is the only deduction on a one-to-one session, and only the first time that client books with you.

| Deduction | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Listing | **\$0** | Forever |
| Introduction fee | **\$70–\$120**, by discipline | Once per new client, ever |
| GST | **10%** on the introduction fee | With that fee |
| Card processing | Paid by the client at checkout | Never taken from you |

<Note>
  Card processing is quoted at Stripe's published Australian domestic rate of **1.7% + 30c**. The
  client is charged that estimate; Stripe's actual cost is read afterwards and any gap is absorbed
  by Open Therapy. It is not deducted from your payout.
</Note>

### The introduction fee

Charged the first time a particular client books with a particular therapist, and never again for that pair. A client who sees two therapists generates two introduction fees.

Fees are quoted ex-GST, with 10% added on top.

| Discipline | Introduction fee |
|---|---|
| Psychiatrist | \$120.00 |
| Psychologist | \$90.00 |
| Clinical Psychologist | \$90.00 |
| Counsellor | \$70.00 |
| Speech Pathologist | \$70.00 |
| Occupational Therapist | \$70.00 |
| Physiotherapist | \$70.00 |
| Any other discipline | \$90.00 |

Two cases where it isn't charged at all:

## A worked example

A psychologist charging **\$180** for a 50-minute session.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="First session with a new client">
    | Line | Amount |
    |---|---|
    | Client pays | \$183.42 |
    | Session fee | \$180.00 |
    | Card processing (client) | \$3.42 |
    | Introduction fee (Psychologist) | −\$90.00 |
    | GST on the introduction fee | −\$9.00 |
    | **You receive** | **\$81.00** |

    Total deducted from the session: \$99.00, or 55.0%.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Every session after that">
    | Line | Amount |
    |---|---|
    | Client pays | \$183.42 |
    | Session fee | \$180.00 |
    | Card processing (client) | \$3.42 |
    | Introduction fee | \$0.00 |
    | **You receive** | **\$180.00** |

    Nothing deducted from the session — card processing was paid by the client.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The rate you actually pay depends on how long clients stay. Figures below are of session fees billed, not of what clients paid including the surcharge:

| | |
|---|---|
| Six sessions with one new client | \$1,080.00 billed, \$99.00 deducted — **9.2%** |
| Twelve sessions with one new client | \$2,160.00 billed, \$99.00 deducted — **4.6%** |

## Group programs are the one place a percentage applies

<Info>
  Group programs are being rolled out. The commission below is what applies once a program can take
  enrolments.
</Info>

A group enrolment carries a flat **15%** commission plus GST. There is no introduction fee on a group place, and card processing is **not** passed through — Open Therapy absorbs Stripe's cost on group enrolments.

One place on a course priced at \$240.00:

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Client pays | \$240.00 |
| Commission (15%) | −\$36.00 |
| GST on the commission | −\$3.60 |
| Card processing | \$0.00 |
| **You receive** | **\$200.40** |

See [Running a group program](/therapists/groups).

## GST

GST is shown as its own line rather than folded into a percentage.

- **The session** is GST-free.
- **Open Therapy's fees** are quoted ex-GST with 10% added on top.
- **Card processing** is paid by the client. On older settlements, where it was recharged to the therapist, it arrived GST-inclusive and its tax was carved back out for the invoice rather than added again.

## Fee changes never re-price the past

Every settlement stores the rates charged at the time it was made. A rate change applies to new sessions only.

The retired **platform fee** line survives for that reason. It was a percentage of every session, and the rate is now zero. It appears only on statements old enough to have paid one, and is never charged on a new session.
