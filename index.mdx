Open Therapy is a directory and booking platform for verified therapists in Australia. You can be seen online, at a clinic, or at home.

Two care types are open for bookings today — **Psychology** and **Counselling**. Twelve more have a page and practitioners behind them, and open one at a time. See [Care types](/care-types).

These pages explain how the platform works from both sides. Pick the one that fits.

## What makes this different

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Matching is built on lived experience, not just modalities" icon="users">
    Most directories let you filter by therapy type and postcode. Open Therapy also asks what you're
    carrying — culture, identity, neurodivergence, grief, caring, faith — and asks therapists which of
    those they have lived through themselves rather than only worked with. The two are kept apart
    because they are different things, and a shared experience is the single heaviest factor in your
    shortlist. See [Find a therapist](/clients/find-a-therapist).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clients pay the therapist's published price, and nothing more" icon="receipt">
    There is no booking fee, no service charge and no surcharge. There is also no percentage taken out
    of a one-to-one session: Open Therapy charges a therapist a one-off introduction fee the first
    time it matches them with a new client, and nothing per session after that. Every fee comes out of
    the therapist's payout, never out of your bill. See [Fees](/fees).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cancellation terms belong to the practice, and are enforced" icon="calendar-x">
    Each therapist chooses one of four cancellation policies. Whatever they choose is what you see on
    their profile, at checkout and in your confirmation email — and it's what the system actually
    charges. The terms are copied onto the session when you book, so a therapist tightening their
    policy later can't re-price an appointment already in your diary. Moving a session is always free.
    See [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reviews describe the service, never the treatment" icon="star">
    Australian law prohibits testimonials about regulated health services. Reviews on Open Therapy can
    cover booking, admin, premises and manner, but not symptoms, treatment or whether therapy helped.
    Every review is screened before it appears, and anything borderline is read by a person rather
    than published. See [Reviews](/clients/reviews).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Everything between sessions is free with an account" icon="headphones">
    There is no subscription on Open Therapy. The whole audio library — a new episode written,
    narrated and published every day — is open to anyone with an account, along with a private
    journal, prompts, and messaging with any therapist in the directory. See
    [Library](/clients/library).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
