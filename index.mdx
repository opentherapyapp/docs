Open Therapy is a directory and booking platform for verified therapists in Australia. Be seen online, at a clinic, or at home.

**Psychology** and **Counselling** are open for bookings today. Twelve more care types have a page and practitioners behind them, and open one at a time. See [Care types](/care-types).

## What makes this different

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Matching is built on lived experience, not just modalities" icon="users">
    Matching asks what you're carrying — culture, identity, neurodivergence, grief, caring, faith —
    and which of those a therapist has lived through rather than only worked with. Shared experience
    is the heaviest factor in your shortlist. See [Find a therapist](/clients/find-a-therapist).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You keep the published session price" icon="receipt">
    No booking fee and no percentage of a one-to-one session. Card processing is added at checkout
    so the therapist keeps the published fee. Open Therapy charges the therapist a one-off
    introduction fee on the first match with a new client, out of their payout. See [Fees](/fees).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cancellation terms belong to the practice, and are enforced" icon="calendar-x">
    Each therapist chooses one of four cancellation policies, shown on their profile, at checkout and
    in your confirmation email. The terms are copied onto the session when you book, and they're what
    you're charged. Moving a session is free. See
    [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reviews describe the service, never the treatment" icon="star">
    Australian law prohibits testimonials about regulated health services. Reviews cover booking,
    admin, premises and manner — not symptoms, treatment or whether therapy helped. Every review is
    screened, and anything borderline is read by a person. See [Reviews](/clients/reviews).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Everything between sessions is free with an account" icon="headphones">
    No subscription. The whole audio library — a new episode every day — is free with an account,
    along with a private journal, prompts, and messaging with any therapist in the directory. See
    [Library](/clients/library).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
