## Crisis support

<Warning>
  Open Therapy is not an emergency service and nobody monitors the platform around the clock. If you
  or someone else is in immediate danger, call **000**.
</Warning>

These Australian services are free and available now.

| Service | Number | For |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency | **000** | Immediate danger |
| Lifeline | **13 11 14** | Crisis support and suicide prevention, 24/7 |
| Suicide Call Back Service | **1300 659 467** | Counselling for anyone affected by suicide |
| 13YARN | **13 92 76** | Crisis support for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people |
| 1800RESPECT | **1800 737 732** | Domestic, family and sexual violence |
| Kids Helpline | **1800 55 1800** | Anyone aged 5 to 25 |
| Beyond Blue | **1300 22 4636** | Anxiety, depression and suicide prevention |
| MensLine Australia | **1300 78 99 78** | Support for men |

## Contacting Open Therapy

There is no support phone line. Everything goes through email, which means every answer is written down and can be re-read.

| Reach | Email | Response |
|---|---|---|
| General support | [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app) | One business day |
| Billing, payouts and invoices | [billing@opentherapy.app](mailto:billing@opentherapy.app) | One business day |
| Clinical governance and conduct | [governance@opentherapy.app](mailto:governance@opentherapy.app) | Acknowledged the same business day |
| Privacy and data requests | [privacy@opentherapy.app](mailto:privacy@opentherapy.app) | Within 30 days |
| Security vulnerabilities | [security@opentherapy.app](mailto:security@opentherapy.app) | — |
| Legal | [legal@opentherapy.app](mailto:legal@opentherapy.app) | — |

<Tip>
  Include your invoice reference — it looks like `OTF-2026-0093` — when you write about a payment. It's
  the fastest way for someone to find the right session.
</Tip>

## Complaints about a practitioner

If your concern is about a registered health practitioner's conduct or clinical care, you can raise it with Open Therapy at [governance@opentherapy.app](mailto:governance@opentherapy.app), and — independently of us — with [Ahpra](https://www.ahpra.gov.au), which regulates registered practitioners nationally.
