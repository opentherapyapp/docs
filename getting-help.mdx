## Crisis support

<Warning>
  Open Therapy is not a crisis service and nobody monitors the platform around the clock. If you or
  someone else is in immediate danger, call **000**.
</Warning>

These Australian services are free. Everything below is staffed 24/7 except MensLine, which publishes its own hours.

| Service | Number | For |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency services | **000** | Immediate danger to life. Police, ambulance, fire |
| Lifeline | **13 11 14** | Crisis support and suicide prevention. Text and chat also available |
| Suicide Call Back Service | **1300 659 467** | Free counselling for anyone affected by suicide |
| 13YARN | **13 92 76** | Crisis support for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people |
| 1800RESPECT | **1800 737 732** | Sexual assault, domestic and family violence counselling |
| Kids Helpline | **1800 55 1800** | Free confidential support for anyone aged 5 to 25 |
| Beyond Blue | **1300 22 4636** | Mental health support and referrals. Phone and web chat |
| MensLine Australia | **1300 78 99 78** | Counselling for men on family, relationship and wellbeing concerns |

The same list is at [opentherapy.app/crisis-support](https://opentherapy.app/crisis-support), and a **Crisis support** link sits in your account menu.

## Contacting Open Therapy

A **Get help** circle sits at the bottom left of most pages. It answers from this help centre — bookings, fees, your account. It is not a therapist and will not advise you on how you're feeling.

Three other ways in all reach the same inbox.

### What to expect

| Topic | What it covers | Reply |
|---|---|---|
| Bookings | Booking or scheduling | Within one business day |
| Billing | Fees, refunds or invoices | Within one business day |
| Clinical Concern | Your care, or your therapist | Within one business day |
| Privacy | Your data, records or account | Within two business days |
| Therapists | Listing, or already listing | Within two business days |
| Something Else | Anything else | Within one business day |

You're emailed a copy of whatever you send.

<Tip>
  Include your invoice reference — it looks like `OTF-2026-0093` — when you write about a payment.
</Tip>

## Reaching your therapist rather than us

Message them from your workspace, under **Messages**. Support doesn't read those threads or answer for your therapist.

Replying to a message notification from your own email inbox lands the reply in the thread.

## Closing your account

Write in and it's closed. Cancel any booked sessions first.

Closing removes your profile, but not clinical notes your therapist is legally required to keep for seven years, or booking and payment records held for the same period. See [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy#your-clinical-record).

## Complaints that go past us

You don't need to come to us first.

| | |
| --- | --- |

## Not urgent, but you need someone soon

Several therapists hold same-week openings, including evenings and online. You don't need a mental health treatment plan to book — bring one to a first session with a psychologist for a Medicare rebate. See [Find a therapist](/clients/find-a-therapist).
