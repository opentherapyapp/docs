## Crisis support

<Warning>
  Open Therapy is not a crisis service and nobody monitors the platform around the clock. If you or
  someone else is in immediate danger, call **000**.
</Warning>

These Australian services are free. Everything below is staffed 24/7 except MensLine, which publishes its own hours.

| Service | Number | For |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency services | **000** | Immediate danger to life. Police, ambulance, fire |
| Lifeline | **13 11 14** | Crisis support and suicide prevention. Text and chat also available |
| Suicide Call Back Service | **1300 659 467** | Free counselling for anyone affected by suicide |
| 13YARN | **13 92 76** | Crisis support for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people |
| 1800RESPECT | **1800 737 732** | Sexual assault, domestic and family violence counselling |
| Kids Helpline | **1800 55 1800** | Free confidential support for anyone aged 5 to 25 |
| Beyond Blue | **1300 22 4636** | Mental health support and referrals. Phone and web chat |
| MensLine Australia | **1300 78 99 78** | Counselling for men on family, relationship and wellbeing concerns |

The same list is on Open Therapy at [opentherapy.app/crisis-support](https://opentherapy.app/crisis-support), and there's a **Crisis support** link in your account menu on every page.

There is one support address, not a directory of them. Everything — bookings, billing, privacy, a concern about a therapist — goes to the same inbox and is routed from there.

### What to expect

The form asks what your message is about, because the answer changes how quickly it's answered. It tells you the same thing on the page before you type.

| Topic | What it covers | Reply |
|---|---|---|
| Bookings | Booking or scheduling | Within one business day |
| Billing | Fees, refunds or invoices | Within one business day |
| Clinical Concern | Your care, or your therapist | Within one business day |
| Privacy | Your data, records or account | Within two business days |
| Therapists | Listing, or already listing | Within two business days |
| Something Else | Anything else | Within one business day |

You're emailed a copy of whatever you send, so you have your own record of it.

<Tip>
  Include your invoice reference — it looks like `OTF-2026-0093` — when you write about a payment. It's
  the fastest way for someone to find the right session.
</Tip>

## Reaching your therapist rather than us

Message them from your workspace, under **Messages**. Those threads are between the two of you, and Open Therapy support doesn't read them or answer for your therapist.

You can also reply to a message notification straight from your own email inbox, and the reply lands in the thread.

## Closing your account

Write in and it's closed. Cancel any sessions you've already booked first, so a therapist isn't left holding the time.

Closing your account removes your profile. It doesn't remove clinical notes your therapist is legally required to keep for seven years, or booking and payment records held for the same period. See [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy#your-clinical-record).

## Complaints that go past us

Two bodies take complaints independently of Open Therapy, and you don't need to come to us first.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| A registered practitioner's conduct or clinical care | [Ahpra](https://www.ahpra.gov.au) |
| How your personal information was handled | [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au) |

## Not urgent, but you need someone soon

Several therapists on Open Therapy hold same-week openings, including evenings and online. Bring a mental health treatment plan to a first session with a psychologist if you want a Medicare rebate — you don't need one to book. See [Find a therapist](/clients/find-a-therapist).
