Open Therapy connects two sides of the same appointment. This page follows one booking from both.

## For a client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer five questions" icon="clipboard-list">
    Tap **Match me** and the questionnaire asks who the therapy is for, what you're bringing, anything
    running through your story, what would make it easier, and the practical constraints — how you'd
    like to meet, where you are, and how you'll pay. Only the first two are required. Naming something
    in your story adds one screen, where you say whether you want someone who has lived it or someone
    who works with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your shortlist" icon="layers">
    Every therapist who scores against your answers is dealt to you as a card, ranked, each with a
    one-line reason. Keep the ones worth a second look and pass on the rest. Kept therapists go to
    your **Shortlist**. Nothing is booked and nobody is told either way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the profile" icon="user">
    Bio, qualifications, registration, focus areas, services and prices, locations, funding
    information, and reviews. You can also skip matching entirely and search the directory yourself
    under **Therapists**, as a list or on a map.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book and pay" icon="calendar-check">
    Pick a service, a session mode, a date and a time. You'll need an account. The therapist's full
    fee is charged to your card at booking — there's no deposit and no part-payment — and it sits with
    Open Therapy until the session has happened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attend" icon="video">
    Online sessions get an encrypted video link that opens in your browser. In-person sessions get the
    address and access notes. Home visits get confirmed arrival details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Three defaults shape what you can pick. A therapist can change any of them, and the times you're shown already account for all three.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Earliest you can book | 24 hours from now |
| Furthest ahead you can book | 2 months |
| Notice needed to move a session | 24 hours |

<Note>
  Moving a session never carries a fee. You need enough notice to move it — your therapist's
  reschedule notice, or their free-cancellation window, whichever is longer — and there's no limit on
  how many times you can. Cancelling is a separate thing with its own terms. See
  [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds).
</Note>

## For a therapist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account and apply" icon="user-plus">
    Sign up as a therapist and work through onboarding in five phases: **Registration** (your care
    type, your title, your registering body and number), **Your practice** (where and how you work,
    and your hours), **Your work** (what you work with, your approaches, your own story), **Fees**,
    then **Your profile** (name, photo, bio, your booking handle and an intro video).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get verified" icon="badge-check">
    Open Therapy checks your registration against the body that holds it. Professions with no board
    are verified on a screening check instead. Nothing goes live until it clears. See
    [Verification](/therapists/verification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live" icon="globe">
    Your profile appears in the directory and in client shortlists where your experience overlaps with
    theirs. You also get a personal booking link at `opentherapy.link/your-handle`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the session" icon="calendar">
    Bookings arrive against the availability you set, in your **Schedule**. The client has already
    paid in full.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record what happened" icon="check">
    Afterwards you mark the session attended or a no-show. This is the step that divides the money:
    nothing is settled and nothing can transfer until you do it. Attendance is your word, not an
    inference from the clock. Unmarked sessions wait for you under **Waiting on you** on your
    **Earnings** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid" icon="banknote">
    Marking the session works out what you're owed. A scheduled pass runs **every hour** and moves
    your share to your connected Stripe account, then Stripe's own payout schedule pays it into your
    bank. See [Set up payouts](/therapists/set-up-payouts).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What has to be true before your share moves

The hourly pass doesn't pay out on trust. Every transfer waits until all of this holds, and a
settlement that isn't ready is picked up by the next pass rather than dropped.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| The session is settled | You've marked it attended or a no-show |
| The client's payment completed | The card actually cleared |
| Stripe's real cost is known | Read off the charge itself, never estimated. A cost that couldn't be read leaves the settlement waiting rather than guessed at |
| Payouts are set up | Your Stripe account can receive transfers |
| Nothing is frozen | A dispute on your account pauses transfers until a person has read it |

<Note>
  There is no fixed delay in hours between a session ending and your money moving. What starts the
  clock is you recording attendance, and the pass that follows runs on the hour.
</Note>

## Who does what

| | Client | Therapist | Open Therapy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sets the session price | — | Yes | No |
| Sets the cancellation policy | — | Yes | No |
| Pays for the session | Yes | — | — |
| Receives the session fee | — | Yes | Deducts its own fees from the payout |
| Merchant on the card statement | — | Yes | No |
| Verifies registration | — | — | Yes |
| Decides the session happened | — | Yes | No |
| Holds the clinical record | — | Yes | Stores it, doesn't read it |

<Note>
  The therapist is the merchant of record on every payment. Their practice name — not Open Therapy —
  is what appears on the client's card statement. Open Therapy earns from a one-off introduction fee
  the first time a client and therapist are matched, and nothing per session after that. See
  [Fees](/fees).
</Note>

## Where you'll be working

Both sides share one signed-in area, at `/workspace`. A client's sidebar groups **Care**, **Between
sessions**, **Finding care** and **Account**; a therapist's groups **Practice**, **Clients**,
**Money** and **Profile**. On a phone the same thing collapses to four tabs and your avatar.

Older links still work. `/account/...` and `/therapist/...` redirect into the workspace, and `/saved`
redirects to `/shortlist`, because both were in email that has already gone out.

## Two domains

Open Therapy runs on two hostnames a person sees, and both are legitimate.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="opentherapy.app" icon="globe">
    Everything: marketing, the directory, profiles, booking, signing in and the workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="opentherapy.link" icon="link">
    Vanity links only. A therapist hands out `opentherapy.link/sarah-chen` and it opens their profile.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## What you can't book yet

Only privately paid sessions can be booked. Bulk-billed, NDIS, WorkCover and DVA bookings are
refused rather than taken and mishandled. Profiles still show what rebates and funding a therapist
accepts, and claiming is arranged between you and them. See
[Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).

Twelve of the fourteen care types are also not open for booking yet. See [Care types](/care-types).
