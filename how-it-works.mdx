This page follows one booking from both sides of the same appointment.

## For a client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer five questions" icon="clipboard-list">
    Tap **Match me**. Who the therapy is for, what you're bringing, anything running through your
    story, what would make it easier, and the practical constraints — how you'd like to meet, where
    you are, how you'll pay. Only the first two are required. Naming something in your story adds one
    screen: someone who has lived it, or someone who works with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your shortlist" icon="layers">
    Every therapist who scores against your answers arrives as a ranked card with a one-line reason.
    Keep the ones worth a second look and they go to your **Shortlist**. Nothing is booked and nobody
    is told either way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the profile" icon="user">
    Bio, qualifications, registration, focus areas, services and prices, locations, funding
    information, and reviews. You can skip matching and search the directory yourself under
    **Therapists**, as a list or on a map.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book and pay" icon="calendar-check">
    Pick a service, a session mode, a date and a time. You'll need an account. The therapist's full
    fee plus card processing is charged at booking — no deposit, no part-payment — and sits with
    Open Therapy until the session has happened. The hour is held for 15 minutes while you pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attend" icon="video">
    Online sessions get an encrypted video link that opens in your browser. In person, you get the
    address and access notes. Home visits get confirmed arrival details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/match-question.webp" width="2880" height="1800" style={{ width: 1440 }} alt="First screen of the matching questionnaire asking Who is the therapy for, with Individual selected and options for Couple, Teen, Child and Someone I care for listed below." />

Three defaults shape the times you're shown. A therapist can change any of them.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Earliest you can book | 24 hours from now |
| Furthest ahead you can book | 2 months |
| Notice needed to move a session | 24 hours |

<Note>
  Moving a session never carries a fee, and there's no limit on how often. You need your therapist's
  reschedule notice or their free-cancellation window, whichever is longer. Cancelling has its own
  terms. See [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds).
</Note>

## For a therapist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account and apply" icon="user-plus">
    Onboarding runs in five phases: **Registration** (care type, title, registering body and number),
    **Your practice** (where and how you work, your hours), **Your work** (what you work with, your
    approaches, your own story), **Fees**, then **Your profile** (name, photo, bio, booking handle,
    intro video).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get verified" icon="badge-check">
    Open Therapy checks your registration against the body that holds it. Professions with no board
    are verified on a screening check instead. Nothing goes live until it clears. See
    [Verification](/therapists/verification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live" icon="globe">
    Your profile appears in the directory, and in client shortlists where your experience overlaps
    with theirs. You also get a personal booking link at `opentherapy.link/your-handle`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the session" icon="calendar">
    Bookings arrive against the availability you set, in your **Schedule**. The client has already
    paid in full.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record what happened" icon="check">
    Mark the session attended or a no-show. Nothing settles and nothing transfers until you do.
    Unmarked sessions wait under **Not closed yet** on your **Earnings** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid" icon="banknote">
    Marking the session works out what you're owed. A pass runs **every hour** and moves your share to
    your connected Stripe account; Stripe's payout schedule pays it into your bank. See
    [Set up payouts](/therapists/set-up-payouts).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What has to be true before your share moves

A transfer waits until all of this holds, and a settlement that isn't ready waits for the next pass.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| The session is settled | You've marked it attended or a no-show |
| The client's payment completed | The card actually cleared |
| Stripe's real cost is known | Read off the charge, never estimated. A cost that can't be read leaves the settlement waiting |
| Payouts are set up | Your Stripe account can receive transfers |
| Nothing is frozen | A dispute on your account pauses transfers until a person has read it |

<Note>
  There is no fixed delay after a session ends. Recording attendance starts the clock.
</Note>

## Who does what

| | Client | Therapist | Open Therapy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sets the session price | — | Yes | No |
| Sets the cancellation policy | — | Yes | No |
| Pays for the session | Yes | — | — |
| Receives the session fee | — | Yes | Deducts its own fees from the payout |
| Merchant on the card statement | — | Yes | No |
| Verifies registration | — | — | Yes |
| Decides the session happened | — | Yes | No |
| Holds the clinical record | — | Yes | Stores it, doesn't read it |

<Note>
  The therapist's practice name is what appears on the client's card statement. Open Therapy earns a
  one-off introduction fee the first time a client and therapist are matched, and nothing per session
  after. See [Fees](/fees).
</Note>

## Where you'll be working

Both sides share one signed-in area, at `/workspace`. On a phone it collapses to four tabs and your
avatar.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| A client's sidebar | **Care**, **Between sessions**, **Finding care**, **Account** |
| A therapist's sidebar | **Practice**, **Clients**, **Money**, **Profile** |

`/account/...` and `/therapist/...` redirect into the workspace, and `/saved` redirects to
`/shortlist`.

## Two domains

Two hostnames, both legitimate.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="opentherapy.app" icon="globe">
    Everything: marketing, the directory, profiles, booking, signing in and the workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="opentherapy.link" icon="link">
    Vanity links only. A therapist hands out `opentherapy.link/sarah-chen` and it opens their profile.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## What you can't book yet

Only privately paid sessions can be booked. Bulk-billed, NDIS, WorkCover and DVA bookings are
refused. Profiles still show what rebates and funding a therapist accepts, and claiming is arranged
between you and them. See [Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).

Nine of the fourteen care types aren't open yet either. See [Care types](/care-types).
