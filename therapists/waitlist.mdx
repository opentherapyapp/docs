Your waitlist is the list of people who wanted a place and couldn't have one yet. It fills itself, and it empties itself when an hour comes free.

## How someone joins

Your **Accepting clients** setting under **Availability** decides what your profile offers:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Clients book directly |
| **Waitlist** | The profile offers the list instead of a booking form |
| **Closed** | Neither. Nobody can book, and nobody can join the list |

Joining takes an email address. A name, preferred times and a short note are optional, and the note is where people say what they're after.

Someone can join without an account. If the address they gave belongs to an Open Therapy account, the entry links to it, and they get an in-app notification alongside the email.

Joining twice does nothing — the same address on the same list is the same entry, and it returns the same confirmation. Someone who was removed and asks again is put back, because asking again is asking again.

## Your list

**Waitlist** in your workspace shows everyone, filtered by status.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Waiting** | Nobody has written to them about an hour |
| **Told about an opening** | They've been notified, and the hour may still be open |
| **Booked from the list** | They took a session after being told |
| **Removed** | They left, or you took them off |
| **All** | Everyone |

Select people and press **Notify** to write to them yourself, with an optional message. Anyone already written to in the last 24 hours is skipped, and you're told how many were skipped rather than being left to wonder.

## When an hour comes free

You don't have to notice a cancellation. When a session is cancelled or moved, the hour it vacated is queued, and an hourly pass offers it to the list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The hour is queued">
    Written the moment the cancellation or reschedule completes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The pass picks it up">
    Within the hour. That delay is deliberate: a therapist who cancelled in order
    to rebook the same hour has usually already rebooked it, and the pass then finds it taken and
    says nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The hour is re-checked">
    Against your live availability, not against what was true when it was queued. An hour that has
    since been taken, blocked out, or fallen outside your booking window is dropped without a word.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Five people are emailed">
    The five longest-waiting on your list who haven't been written to recently.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Five, and first come first asked

An hour is one appointment, and the email says so — whoever books it first has it.

Telling forty people turns one piece of good news into thirty-nine disappointments and a race. Telling five keeps it a queue. On a list long enough for that to matter, the five longest-waiting are the fairest five to ask, so the order is the order they joined.

### How long an offer stands

There is no hold and no expiry timer on an individual offer. The hour is on the market from the moment the mail goes out, and it belongs to whoever books it.

What is bounded is how often someone hears from you. Nobody is written to about an opening more than once in **24 hours**, and nobody is written to twice about the same hour.

### When nobody is told

An opening is closed without a send if the hour has already passed, if you've since closed your books, or if it's no longer available. Closing your books is a setting that says nobody can book — advertising an hour out of it would contradict you.

## Leaving

Every waitlist email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. It removes the entry immediately, with no sign-in, no confirmation step and no account required. The link works once; after that it reads as expired.

You can also remove someone from your list. They aren't told.

## The care-category waitlist is a different list

Some care types are marked coming soon on the site. Those pages collect email addresses too, and that list is **not yours**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Your waitlist** | People waiting for you. Yours to see and to notify |
| **Care-category waitlist** | People waiting for a care type to launch. Held by Open Therapy, and emailed once when it does |

Nobody on the care-category list appears in your workspace, and joining one has no effect on the other. See [Care types](/care-types).
