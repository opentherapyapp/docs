## When a booking arrives

You get a **New booking** email. If it's a client's first session with you it reads *"A new client booked in"*, and if they've seen you before, *"Another session booked."*

A first booking carries a line about the introduction fee: *"New client — an introduction fee applies to this booking. It appears on your next invoice, once."* Once, not on every session after it. See [Fees and earnings](/therapists/fees-and-earnings).

The client has already paid. Their money sits with Open Therapy until the session is closed off.

## Your day

**Overview** opens on **Today**: each session with its time, the client, the service and the mode. Two buttons sit on each row — **Prepare** takes you into the client's file, and **Join** appears on an online session from fifteen minutes before it starts until its end time. **Open next room** at the top does the same thing without you having to find the row.

Underneath, **Needs you** lists what's outstanding: sessions in the last fortnight with no note against them, a payout setup left unfinished, no hours published, and messages waiting for a reply. Tick one off and it stays off.

## Your schedule

**Schedule** draws the diary as a grid, in day, week or month view. Four things appear on it:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sessions** | The client's name and the mode. Click one to open their file. Past sessions stay on the grid, set back rather than removed |
| **Unopened hours** | Time outside your weekly pattern, shaded so you can see the shape of what you've published |
| **Busy** | Events mirrored from a [connected calendar](/therapists/connected-calendar). Nobody sees what they are — only that you're unavailable |
| **Block-outs** | One-off time you've closed. Click to edit or remove |

Click any empty stretch to block it out, and **New appointment** to book one yourself.

## Booking an appointment yourself

**New appointment** on Schedule, or **Add appointment** on Overview.

Choose the client — an existing one, or file a record for someone you already see off-platform. Then the service, the day and time, and whether it's online or at one of your locations.

The fee defaults to the service's listed price. You can reduce it, or set it to zero, but not raise it: *"Reduce it or set it to zero — you can't charge more than the service lists."*

### Repeat bookings

Tick **Repeat this appointment** to book the same hour on the same weekday ahead — weekly or fortnightly, up to twelve occurrences. Any date already busy is skipped and named, so you don't lose the series to one clash.

On a repeat booking with a fee, the client is asked to pay for the first session now and for each of the others the week before it. Nobody is charged months in advance for a course of therapy they might not finish.

### Asking for payment

**Email them a payment request** sends a link. The hour is held and confirms itself the moment they pay, and nothing is charged before that. For a client filing in from off-platform, they set up their account from the link and then pay.

The option needs two things: a finished payout setup and an email address to send to. Without either you can still book the session and take payment your own way.

You can also add a private note to the appointment — *"What this one is for. Only you see it."*

## Recording attendance

This is the one action that matters most, because **nothing settles and nothing transfers until the session is closed off.**

After a session has finished it appears in **Waiting on you** on your earnings screen, described as *"Not paid out until you mark each session complete."* Two buttons:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Mark complete" icon="check">
    The session happened. Fees are itemised, the client's invoice is issued, and your share is queued
    for transfer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No-show" icon="user-x">
    The client didn't turn up. Your cancellation policy's no-show rate is charged and the rest is
    refunded.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Pressing a button twice is safe. Every write is idempotent, so a double-click can't settle a session twice or invoice a client twice.

<Warning>
  **Record a no-show before the session closes itself.** A confirmed session whose end time passed more
  than 24 hours ago is closed automatically by an hourly pass, and a session that has already been held
  can't be cancelled — which is what a no-show is. Once it closes, the option is gone.
</Warning>

### Why the automatic close exists

A client's ability to claim a Medicare rebate shouldn't depend on their therapist doing admin, and the invoice is raised when the session closes. So the pass closes anything the hour has clearly passed for, and **Mark complete** is a way to do it sooner rather than the only way.

A full day of grace is deliberate: short enough that a client isn't waiting on paperwork to claim, long enough that a therapist who means to record a no-show has a whole working day to do it.

### Why a session might refuse to close

| Message | Reason |
|---|---|
| *"You can close this once the session has finished."* | Its end time hasn't passed |
| *"A cancelled session can't be settled."* | It was called off |
| *"Only the therapist who ran the session can close it."* | It isn't yours |

The timing check exists so nobody can be paid for next Tuesday.

### Waiting on a Stripe fee

A second panel, *"Marked complete. We ask Stripe again every hour — nothing for you to do."*

Your share is worked out from the real card processing fee, so it can't be estimated while Stripe hasn't stated what the charge cost. Nothing is lost — the split and the transfer happen as soon as it arrives. **Ask now** asks early if you'd rather not wait for the hour.

## Cancelling and rescheduling

A client can cancel or move their own session from **Sessions**, on the terms of [your cancellation policy](/therapists/cancellation-policy). Moving is always free; cancelling may not be.

There's no cancel or move button on your side of a booked session yet. Message the client and ask them to move it, which costs them nothing at any notice a move is allowed at, or write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app) if the hour has to come off the diary. A cancellation recorded as yours never charges the client, whatever the notice — that isn't a setting, and it's deliberately not one.

<Tip>
  If a client asks to move a session inside your notice window, a move is nearly always the better
  answer than a cancellation: it costs them nothing and leaves you with the hour sold.
</Tip>

Either way you're emailed. A client cancellation that carried a fee says so: *"Under your cancellation policy, a fee of \$90.00 is retained and will settle with your other sessions."* One that didn't says *"No cancellation fee applies. The hour is free again."*

When an hour comes free before its start time, anyone [waiting](/therapists/waitlist) is told.
