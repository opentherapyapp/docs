## When a booking arrives

You get a **New booking** email — *"A new client booked in"* for a client's first session with you, *"Another session booked"* after that.

A first booking carries a line about the introduction fee: *"New client — an introduction fee applies to this booking. It appears on your next invoice, once."* See [Fees and earnings](/therapists/fees-and-earnings).

The client has already paid. Their money sits with Open Therapy until the session is closed off.

## Your day

**Overview** opens on **Today**: each session with its time, the client, the service and the mode.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prepare** | Opens the client's file |
| **Room** | On an online session once a room exists, before you can walk in |
| **Join** | Replaces Room from fifteen minutes before the start until the end time |
| **Open next room** | The same, from the top of the page |

**Needs you** lists sessions in the last fortnight with no note against them, a payout setup left unfinished, no hours published, and messages waiting for a reply. Tick one off and it stays off.

## Your schedule

**Schedule** draws the diary as a grid, in day, week or month view. Four things appear on it:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sessions** | The client's name and the mode. Click one to open their file. Past sessions stay on the grid, set back rather than removed |
| **Unopened hours** | Time outside your weekly pattern, shaded so you can see the shape of what you've published |
| **Busy** | Events mirrored from a [connected calendar](/therapists/connected-calendar). Nobody sees what they are — only that you're unavailable |
| **Block-outs** | One-off time you've closed. Click to edit or remove |

A checkout hold is drawn separately from a paid session, and the heading counts them apart — *"5 sessions booked this week."* and, when one is held, *"1 hour is held awaiting payment."* Free meet-and-greets are counted on their own line rather than folded into booked sessions. Week view can be limited to **Hours shown**, and split by place when you work at more than one.

Click any empty stretch to block it out, **New appointment** to book one.

## Booking an appointment yourself

**New appointment** on Schedule, or **Add appointment** on Overview. Choose the client — an existing one, or a record you file for someone you see off-platform — then the service, the day and time, and whether it's online or at one of your locations.

The fee defaults to the service's listed price. Reduce it or set it to zero, but you can't charge more than the service lists.

Add a private note to the appointment if you want one. Only you see it.

### Repeat bookings

Tick **Repeat this appointment** for the same hour on the same weekday ahead — weekly or fortnightly, up to twelve occurrences. Any date already busy is skipped and named.

With a fee, the client pays for the first session now and for each of the others the week before it.

### Asking for payment

**Email them a payment request** sends a link. The hour is held and confirms itself the moment they pay. Someone coming from off-platform sets up their account from the link, then pays.

The option needs a finished payout setup and an email address. Without either, book the session and take payment your own way.

## Recording attendance

**Nothing settles and nothing transfers until the session is closed off.** A finished session appears in **Not closed yet** on your earnings screen, with two buttons:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Mark complete" icon="check">
    The session happened. Fees are itemised, the client's invoice is issued, and your share is queued
    for transfer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No-show" icon="user-x">
    The client didn't turn up. Your cancellation policy's no-show rate is charged and the rest is
    refunded.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Pressing a button twice is safe: every write is idempotent.

<Warning>
  **Record a no-show before the session closes itself.** An hourly pass closes any confirmed session
  whose end time passed more than 24 hours ago, and a session that has already been held can't be
  cancelled — which is what a no-show is. Once it closes, the option is gone.
</Warning>

### Why the automatic close exists

The invoice is raised when the session closes, so **Mark complete** closes a session sooner rather than being the only way to close it.

### Why a session might refuse to close

| Message | Reason |
|---|---|
| *"You can close this once the session has finished."* | Its end time hasn't passed |
| *"A cancelled session can't be settled."* | It was called off |
| *"Only the therapist who ran the session can close it."* | It isn't yours |

### Fees still pending

A closed session still waits for Stripe to state what the charge cost before it settles. You'll see *"The session is closed, but its fees are still pending."* Stripe is asked again every hour.

## Cancelling and rescheduling

A client can cancel or move their own session from **Sessions**, on the terms of [your cancellation policy](/therapists/cancellation-policy). Moving is always free; cancelling may not be.

There's no cancel or move button on your side yet. Ask the client to move it, or write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app) if the hour has to come off the diary. A cancellation recorded as yours never charges the client, whatever the notice.

<Tip>
  A move inside your notice window beats a cancellation: it costs the client nothing and leaves you
  with the hour sold.
</Tip>

Either way you're emailed. A cancellation that carried a fee says *"Under your cancellation policy, a fee of \$90.00 is retained and will settle with your other sessions."* One that didn't says *"No cancellation fee applies. The hour is free again."*

When an hour comes free before its start time, anyone [waiting](/therapists/waitlist) is told.
