Clients book the hours you publish at **Availability** in your workspace. A verified therapist with no hours published can't be booked.

## Taking new clients

| | |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Your profile shows your times and takes bookings |
| **Waitlist** | Your profile collects details instead of bookings |
| **Closed** | Your profile stays up. Nobody can book or join a list |

<Note>
  Anyone you tell about an opening can book, whichever of these is set. See
  [Your booking link](/therapists/your-booking-link).
</Note>

Switch to **Waitlist** rather than disappearing when you're full — you keep your place in the directory. See [Waitlist](/therapists/waitlist).

## Your timezone

Set under **Settings**, not on this page. Every hour you publish is written in that zone, and clients see them converted to their own.

## Weekly pattern

One row per day. Turn a day on, add one or more blocks of hours to it, and — if you have more than one location — say which place that day is at. A therapist with no locations works online, and the row says **Online**.

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday | 9:00 – 13:00, 14:00 – 18:00 |
| Tuesday | 10:00 – 17:00 |
| Wednesday | 9:00 – 14:00 |
| Thursday | 11:00 – 19:00 |
| Friday | 9:00 – 15:00 |
| Saturday, Sunday | Off |

Each row saves on its own, and the pattern applies from next Monday.

<Tip>
  Split blocks rather than opening a single long day.
</Tip>

## Booking rules

Four numbers, with the defaults below applying until you change them.

| Rule | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum notice** | Clients can't book a slot starting sooner than this | 24 hours |
| **Booking horizon** | How far ahead your calendar is open to new bookings | 2 months |
| **Reschedule notice** | How much warning a client needs to give to move an appointment | 24 hours |
| **Gap between sessions** | Time kept free either side of an appointment for notes and a breath | 0 minutes, shown as **Back to back** |

Minimum notice and reschedule notice each accept up to 168 hours. The horizon runs from 1 to 12 months, and the gap from 0 to 60 minutes.

Reschedule notice is not a cancellation window. The notice a client needs to move a session is whichever of the two is longer. See [Cancellation policy](/therapists/cancellation-policy#rescheduling).

## Cancellation policy

Chosen on this screen from four presets, and it saves the moment you pick one. The lines under the cards are the exact lines a client is shown before they book and in their confirmation email.

<Warning>
  A session booked before you've chosen a policy carries **no cancellation fee at all** — not for a late
  cancellation and not for a no-show.
</Warning>

Existing bookings keep the policy they were booked under. Changes apply to new bookings. See [Cancellation policy](/therapists/cancellation-policy).

## Your schedule

**Schedule** draws your diary as a grid, in day, week or month view, with a stepper and a **Today** button. The header gives the sessions booked in the span and the timezone it's drawn in.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sessions** | Booked sessions, with the mode. Click one to open that client's file |
| **Unopened hours** | Hours your weekly pattern never opened, drawn through |
| **Busy** | Hours held by a calendar you've connected. It goes when the event does |
| **Block-outs** | Hours you closed yourself |

Past sessions stay on the grid.

### One-off exceptions

Click an empty cell, or drag down a few:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Block out** | Nobody can book these hours, even if you normally work them |
| **Open these hours** | Bookable this once, without changing your weekly pattern |

A block-out takes an optional reason — supervision, leave, admin — and clicking an existing one lets you remove it. Your weekly pattern is still edited on the availability page.

<Tip>
  **New appointment** on the schedule books a client in directly, including a repeat series. See
  [Managing sessions](/therapists/managing-sessions#booking-a-client-in-yourself).
</Tip>

## Connected calendar and calendar feed

You can connect an outside calendar so its events hold hours here, and subscribe to your Open Therapy sessions from elsewhere with **Calendar feed**. See [Connected calendar](/therapists/connected-calendar).
