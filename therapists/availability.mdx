Clients see and book the hours you publish at **Availability** in your workspace. Nothing else on your profile opens a booking — a verified therapist with no hours published can't be booked.

## Taking new clients

Three states, and this is the one that decides what your hours are for.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Open to new clients** | Your profile shows your times and takes bookings |
| **Waitlist only** | Your profile collects details instead of bookings |
| **Not taking anyone** | Your profile stays up. Nobody can book or join a list |

<Note>
  Anyone you tell about an opening can book, whichever of these is set. Closing the front door doesn't
  close your booking link. See [Your booking link](/therapists/your-booking-link).
</Note>

Better to switch to **Waitlist only** than to disappear when you're full. You keep your place in the directory, and you're not starting from zero when you reopen. See [Waitlist](/therapists/waitlist).

## Your timezone

Set directly above your hours, because "9:00 AM" means nothing without it. Every hour you publish is written in this zone, and clients see them converted to their own.

## Weekly pattern

One row per day. Turn a day on, add one or more blocks of hours to it, and — if you have more than one location — say which place that day is at. A therapist with no locations works online, and the row says **Online**.

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday | 9:00 – 13:00, 14:00 – 18:00 |
| Tuesday | 10:00 – 17:00 |
| Wednesday | 9:00 – 14:00 |
| Thursday | 11:00 – 19:00 |
| Friday | 9:00 – 15:00 |
| Saturday, Sunday | Off |

Each row saves on its own, and the panel says the pattern applies from next Monday.

<Tip>
  Split blocks rather than opening a single long day. A gap at lunch that exists in your calendar is
  better than one you have to defend by declining a booking.
</Tip>

## Booking rules

Four numbers, and the defaults are what applies until you change them.

| Rule | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum notice** | Clients can't book a slot starting sooner than this | 24 hours |
| **Booking horizon** | How far ahead your calendar is open to new bookings | 2 months |
| **Reschedule notice** | How much warning a client needs to give to move an appointment | 24 hours |
| **Gap between sessions** | Time kept free either side of an appointment for notes and a breath | 0 minutes, shown as **Back to back** |

Minimum notice and reschedule notice each accept up to 168 hours. The horizon runs from 1 to 12 months, and the gap from 0 to 60 minutes.

<Note>
  A gap is the cheapest protection against a day that runs over. Ten minutes either side turns six
  back-to-back appointments into a day you can write notes in. It defaults to zero because that's what
  the engine always did, and some therapists publish back-to-back on purpose.
</Note>

Reschedule notice is not a cancellation window. Cancellation terms come from the policy preset below, and the notice a client actually needs to move a session is whichever of the two is longer. See [Cancellation policy](/therapists/cancellation-policy#rescheduling).

## Cancellation policy

Chosen on this screen, from four presets, and it saves the moment you pick one. The lines you see under the cards are the exact lines a client is shown before they book and in their confirmation email, generated from the policy the database will charge against.

<Warning>
  A session booked before you've chosen a policy carries **no cancellation fee at all** — not for a late
  cancellation and not for a no-show. A fee nobody chose isn't one we'll collect on your behalf.
</Warning>

Existing bookings keep the policy they were booked under. Changes apply to new bookings. See [Cancellation policy](/therapists/cancellation-policy).

## Your schedule

**Schedule** draws your diary as a grid, in day, week or month view, with a stepper to page through and a **Today** button to come back. The header tells you how many sessions are booked in the span you're looking at, and the timezone it's drawn in.

Four things appear on the grid:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sessions** | Booked appointments, with the mode. Click one to open that client's file |
| **Unopened hours** | Hours your weekly pattern never opened, drawn through |
| **Busy** | Hours held by a calendar you've connected. Nothing to act on — it goes when the event does |
| **Block-outs** | Hours you closed yourself. The one kind of closed hour there's something to do about |

Past sessions stay on the grid and sit back rather than disappearing. An empty Monday isn't a week anyone recognises.

### One-off exceptions

Click an empty cell, or drag down a few, and you're offered two things:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Block out** | Nobody can book these hours, even if you normally work them |
| **Open these hours** | Bookable this once, without changing your weekly pattern |

A block-out takes an optional reason — supervision, leave, admin — and clicking an existing one lets you remove it. Your weekly pattern is still edited on the availability page; this is for the exceptions, which is what a calendar is for.

<Tip>
  **New appointment** on the schedule books a client in directly, including a repeat series. See
  [Managing sessions](/therapists/managing-sessions#booking-a-client-in-yourself).
</Tip>

## Connected calendar and calendar feed

You can connect an outside calendar so its events hold hours here, and subscribe to your Open Therapy sessions from elsewhere with **Calendar feed**. See [Connected calendar](/therapists/connected-calendar).
