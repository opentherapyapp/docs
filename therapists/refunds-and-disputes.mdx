## Refunds

A refund happens in two steps, in this order: the client is refunded first, because it's their money, and then your transfer is reversed if one had already been made.

Each step can succeed or fail independently, which is deliberate — a failure in the second step doesn't leave a client without their refund.

### Card processing on a refund

Stripe keeps its fee when money goes back. Its real cost, taken against the full price, can be larger than the cancellation fee it's now being deducted from.

<Note>
  Processing is clamped to what the session can carry. On a refunded session you net **zero** rather
  than owing us the difference.
</Note>

## Failed reversals

Sometimes Stripe has already paid your balance out to your bank before a refund is issued. The reversal then fails, the client's refund stands, and the shortfall becomes a **receivable** recorded against your account.

This isn't treated as an error to retry. It's a debt for a person to work out with you, and you can see anything you're said to owe — somebody should be able to see what they're being told they owe.

## Disputes

A dispute — a chargeback — is a client's bank pulling a payment back.

<Warning>
  A dispute **freezes all transfers** on your account until a person clears it. Nothing thaws
  automatically. If a payout you expected hasn't arrived, this is the first thing to check.
</Warning>

If your share of the disputed session had already been transferred, it's recorded as a receivable.

Open Therapy holds the payment losses on disputes, but the freeze is not a judgement about you — it's a hold while the facts are established.

### If your account is frozen

Write to [billing@opentherapy.app](mailto:billing@opentherapy.app) with the session details. Include anything relevant: appointment records, correspondence with the client, and your attendance record for the session.

## Reducing disputes before they happen

Most disputes are a client not recognising a charge, not a client contesting the care.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your name is on the statement" icon="credit-card">
    You're the merchant of record, so your practice name appears — not Open Therapy. Make sure the
    name a client sees at booking matches the one on your Stripe account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Choose a policy and keep it visible" icon="calendar-x">
    Cancellation fees are the most disputed charges. The policy is quoted at checkout and in the
    confirmation email, which is exactly the evidence that resolves it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Record attendance promptly" icon="check">
    An attendance record contemporaneous with the session is stronger than one added a fortnight later.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Answer messages" icon="message-circle">
    A client who can reach you asks you before asking their bank.
  </Card>
</Columns>
