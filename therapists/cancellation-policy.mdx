Choose your cancellation policy on **Availability**. It saves the moment you pick a card.

## The four presets

| Policy | Free cancellation | Inside the window | Did not attend |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flexible** | 12 hours | No fee | 50% |
| **Standard** | 24 hours | 50% | Full fee |
| **Firm** | 48 hours | 50% | Full fee |
| **Strict** | 48 hours | Full fee | Full fee |

## Choose one before you take bookings

<Warning>
  A session booked before you've chosen a policy carries **no fee for anything** — no late fee and no
  no-show fee.
</Warning>

Terms are copied onto each session as it's booked. A change applies to new bookings only, and a session already in the diary keeps the terms it was booked under.

## Three rules that aren't yours to set

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="You never charge for your own cancellation" icon="shield-check">
    If you cancel, the client is refunded in full regardless of notice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cancelling after the start time is a no-show" icon="clock">
    A client who cancels once the session was due to begin pays the no-show rate, not the late rate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The boundary itself is free" icon="equal">
    Notice is measured as "at least". Exactly 24 hours before a Standard session costs nothing.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Only you can record a no-show. A session can only be cancelled once, and a second attempt is refused.

## Rescheduling

Moving a session is free, with no cap on the number of moves. The notice a client needs is:

**max(your reschedule notice, the free-cancellation window of the policy the session was booked under)**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Standard, 24-hour notice** | A client needs 24 hours to move |
| **Firm, 24-hour notice** | 48 hours, because the policy's window is longer |
| **Flexible, 24-hour notice** | 24 hours, because yours is longer |

Inside the threshold the hour can't be moved, only cancelled: *"Moving needs 24 hours notice. You can still cancel, under your therapist's policy."*

A session that was due to start can't be moved at all — *"This session was due to start, so it can't be moved."*

<Note>
  You can always move your own appointment, whatever the notice.
</Note>

## What the client sees

The wording is generated from the terms in force. On **Standard**, a client reads three rows:

| | |
|---|---|
| More than 24 hours | Cancel or reschedule yourself, no fee, no conversation needed |
| Inside 24 hours | 50% of the session fee, charged to the card on file |
| Did not attend | Full fee. Rebates can't be claimed on a missed session |

On **Flexible** they read two:

| | |
|---|---|
| Any time before it starts | Cancel or reschedule yourself, whenever you need to. No fee, nothing to explain |
| Did not attend | 50% of the fee. Rebates can't be claimed on a missed session |

In one line: Standard is *"Free cancellation up to 24 hours before. Inside that, 50% of the fee, and the full fee for a session you don't attend."*, Flexible *"Cancel whenever you need to, no fee. A missed session is charged at 50%."*

Before confirming, a client is told the exact amount — *"\$90.00 late cancellation fee. The remaining \$90.00 goes back on your card."* They can also give a reason, up to 500 characters, which reaches you.

## What you receive

A cancellation fee settles like any other earning: the fee becomes the gross. Card processing is not taken from that — you keep the retained session fee.

The client paid the session plus card processing, so a refund includes a matching share of that surcharge. The dialog quotes the session amounts — *"\$90.00 late cancellation fee. The remaining \$90.00 goes back on your card."* — and the card is then credited the session remainder plus its share of processing.

### Worked example

A \$180.00 session on **Standard**, returning client, cancelled with five hours' notice. The client paid **\$183.42**.

The fee is 50% of \$180.00, so \$90.00 is retained. \$91.71 goes back on the card (\$90.00 of the session plus \$1.71 of the surcharge). That \$90.00 settles as its own gross:

| | |
|---|---|
| Cancellation fee retained | \$90.00 |
| **You receive** | **\$90.00** |

With no notice, or a no-show, the full session fee applies and you receive \$180.00.

| Client cancels | You're paid | Client refunded |
|---|---|---|
| 3 days before | \$0.00 | \$183.42 |
| 5 hours before | \$90.00 | \$91.71 |
| Doesn't attend | \$180.00 | \$0.00 |
| You cancel | \$0.00 | \$183.42 |

<Note>
  Those figures assume a returning client, so no introduction fee. On a client's first settled session
  the introduction fee comes off too, clamped so your payout can't go below zero. See
  [Fees and earnings](/therapists/fees-and-earnings#the-two-clamps).
</Note>
