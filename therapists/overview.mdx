Open Therapy is a directory and booking platform for verified practitioners in Australia. You keep your own fees, cancellation terms and clinical judgement.

## What it costs

**Free to list**, with no monthly fee, no minimum and no percentage on a one-to-one session.

| | |
|---|---|
| Listing | **\$0** forever |
| Introduction fee | **One-off** per new client, \$70–\$120 ex-GST by discipline |
| Card processing | Added at checkout, paid by the client |
| GST | **10%** on our introduction fee |
| Group enrolments | **15%** commission plus GST |

The introduction fee is charged the first time a client books with you, never again for that pair. A returning session has nothing taken from it — you keep the published price. Card processing is added to what the client pays.

See [Fees](/fees) and [Fees and earnings](/therapists/fees-and-earnings) for worked examples.

## What stays yours

| Decision | Who makes it |
|---|---|
| Your session fees | You |
| Which services you offer, and their length | You |
| Your cancellation policy | You — from four presets |
| Your hours, and how far ahead they open | You |
| Whether you're taking new clients | You |
| Whether a session was attended | You |
| Whether a client is right for you | You |

Two rules are ours: **you never charge a client for a session you cancelled yourself**, and **a client who cancels after the start time pays the no-show rate rather than the late rate**. See [Cancellation policy](/therapists/cancellation-policy).

## Who can list

Practitioners registered with Ahpra, Speech Pathology Australia, Dietitians Australia, PACFA or ACA, ESSA, ANZACATA or AMTA, plus support and developmental education roles where no board exists. See [Verification](/therapists/verification).

<Info>
  Five care categories are open for booking so far: **Psychology**, **Counselling**, **Social
  Workers**, **Mental Health Support Workers**, and **Art & Music Therapy**. The other eleven have a
  landing page and take waitlist interest, and profiles in them are reachable by link, but they aren't
  in the directory and can't be booked yet. You can apply now either way. See
  [Care types](/care-types).
</Info>
