Open Therapy is a directory and booking platform for verified practitioners in Australia. You keep your own fees, your own cancellation terms and your own clinical judgement. We handle the listing, the matching, the booking and the money.

## What it costs

**Free to list.** No monthly fee, no seat licence, no minimum. There is no percentage taken from a one-to-one session.

| | |
|---|---|
| Listing | **\$0** forever |
| Introduction fee | **One-off** per new client, \$70–\$120 ex-GST by discipline |
| Card processing | Stripe's actual cost, passed through |
| GST | **10%** on our introduction fee |
| Group enrolments | **15%** commission plus GST |

The introduction fee is charged once, the first time a particular client books with you, and never again for that pair. So every returning session costs you nothing beyond card processing — around \$3.36 on a \$180 session paid by an Australian domestic card.

Clients who arrive through your own booking link carry no introduction fee at all. See [Fees](/fees) for the canonical breakdown, and [Fees and earnings](/therapists/fees-and-earnings) for worked examples and how to read your earnings screen.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Sign up as a therapist and work through the setup — see [Join Open Therapy](/therapists/join).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get verified">
    We check your registration with the body you nominated. See
    [Verification](/therapists/verification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up payouts">
    Connect a Stripe account. Clients can't check out until it's active — see
    [Set up payouts](/therapists/set-up-payouts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your hours">
    Set your weekly pattern, choose a cancellation policy, and start appearing in the directory. See
    [Availability](/therapists/availability).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What stays yours

| Decision | Who makes it |
|---|---|
| Your session fees | You |
| Which services you offer, and their length | You |
| Your cancellation policy | You — from four presets |
| Your hours, and how far ahead they open | You |
| Whether you're taking new clients | You |
| Whether a session was attended | You |
| Whether a client is right for you | You |

Two rules are ours rather than yours, and both are deliberate: **you never charge a client for a session you cancelled yourself**, and **a client who cancels after the start time pays the no-show rate rather than the late rate**. See [Cancellation policy](/therapists/cancellation-policy).

## Who can list

Practitioners registered with Ahpra, Speech Pathology Australia, Dietitians Australia, PACFA or ACA, ESSA, ANZACATA or AMTA — plus support and developmental education roles where no board exists, which are checked differently. See [Verification](/therapists/verification).

<Info>
  Two care categories are open for booking so far: **Psychology** and **Counselling**. The other twelve
  have a landing page and take waitlist interest, and profiles in them are reachable by link, but they
  aren't in the directory and can't be booked yet. You can apply now either way. See
  [Care types](/care-types).
</Info>
