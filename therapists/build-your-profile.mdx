Your profile is everything a client reads before booking. Build and edit it in **Set up your practice** — **Edit my answers** reopens the flow with your answers still in it.

## What a client sees

| Section | Comes from |
|---|---|
| **About** | Your bio, your session modes and your languages |
| **Services** | Each service you've priced |
| **How I work** | Your approaches and modalities |
| **Blog** | Your published posts, if you write |
| **Groups** | Any group programs you run |
| **Location** | Each practice location you've added |
| **Focus areas** | What you work with |
| **Has lived** | Lived experiences, if you've chosen to publish them |
| **Also works with** | Experiences you work with clinically |
| **Qualifications** | Your registering body, years practising and qualifications |
| **Rebates and funding** | The funding you accept, and your sliding scale if you have one |
| **Reviews** | Reviews that have passed screening |

Beside the booking panel sit two assurances: **"Registration checked by Open Therapy"** and, for online sessions, **"Encrypted video link for online sessions"**.

## Your name and how you're addressed

Your name is prefilled from signup. Correct it here to the form you practise under.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pronouns** | Optional. Shown in the line under your name, beside your discipline |
| **Gender** | Optional, and never printed. It answers a client who is searching for one |

## Writing a bio

At least 40 characters to save, up to 900. The prompt asks for who you work with, how a session goes, and what to expect in the first few weeks.

## Lived experience and what you work with

Eight themes cover the ground: culture, migration and belonging; family and the people who raised you; identity, gender and sexuality; neurodivergence, disability and health; faith, meaning and community; work, money and study; parenting, fertility and caring; and loss, harm and survival.

Pick the themes you hold. Each adds a short screen of specific experiences, where you can mark one of two things.

| | |
|---|---|
| **I've lived this** | You have been through it yourself |
| **I work with it** | You work with it clinically |

Leave anything that's neither blank. The two are captured separately and appear under different headings on your profile: **Has lived** — *"has been through these, not only trained in them"* — and **Also works with**.

<Warning>
  Only mark something as lived if it's true. It is the basis on which a client chooses you.
</Warning>

### Publishing the lived list, or keeping it back

Once you've marked at least one experience as lived, you choose **Keep to matching** or **Show on my profile**.

<Note>
  Matching works either way. Publishing shows them to every visitor; keeping them to matching shows
  them only to clients who named the same thing. An experience marked both lived and worked with still
  appears under **Also works with**.
</Note>

### In your own words

An optional paragraph of up to 600 characters, shown to clients who named the same things. Something like: *"I grew up as the family's translator and know the grief of a culture you were never taught. You won't need to give me the background."*

## Photo and reel

    Optional. MP4, WebM or MOV, up to 60 seconds. Filmed on a phone is fine.

The reel screen suggests three things: who you tend to work with, what a first session feels like, and one thing you'd want someone nervous to know.

<Tip>
  Encoding takes a minute or so, and you can submit without waiting. The reel joins your profile when
  it's ready.
</Tip>

## Focus areas and approaches

**What you work with** is what you'd take a referral for tomorrow. It feeds your focus areas, the directory filters and matching.

**Your approaches and modalities** are a separate list, shown under **How I work**. Write in one that isn't there and a phrase we recognise selects the existing option rather than creating a duplicate.

Be specific rather than exhaustive. A list of thirty doesn't rank you any higher.

## Locations and how you work

Session modes are **Online**, **In person** and **Home visit**, summarised on your profile in a line — *"Online + in person"*.

If you see people in person, add every place a client might turn up to: a name, a street address, a suburb, a city, a state and a postcode, plus three things worth filling in:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Step-free access** | Level entry, an accessible bathroom and a bay to park in |
| **Getting here** | *"Five minutes from Newtown station"* |
| **Parking** | *"Metered street parking, free after 6pm"* |

There's no cap on locations, and location matching covers every place you work from.

Changing a location doesn't send your listing back for review.

You also set your timezone here, and clients see every time converted to their own. Name the languages you can hold a session in — conversational counts if you'd be comfortable doing the work in it.
