Every profile on Open Therapy has had its practitioner checked before going live. The directory only shows a profile that is both published and verified, so an application that hasn't cleared is invisible everywhere.

## What's checked

The number you gave, against the register or membership roll held by the body you nominated.

| Body | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Ahpra** | Psychology, psychiatry, occupational therapy, physiotherapy, osteopathy, chiropractic, podiatry |
| **Speech Pathology Australia** | Speech pathology |
| **Dietitians Australia** | Dietetics and nutrition |
| **PACFA** or **ACA** | Counselling and psychotherapy |
| **ESSA** | Exercise physiology |
| **ANZACATA** | Art therapy |
| **AMTA** | Music therapy |
| **Something else, or none** | Support work and developmental education |

Three things follow from that list.

**Ahpra is the register, where there is one.** The professions it covers are registered under the National Law, and their registration is a matter of public record we can check directly.

**Speech pathology and counselling are not registered professions**, so there is no national register to check. What exists instead is membership of a professional association with its own entry standards, and that is what we check.

**Some roles have no board at all.** A support worker or developmental educator has nothing to give a registration number for. They're still verified, on a worker screening check rather than a register, and their profile carries that wording instead — **"Worker screening checked by Open Therapy"** rather than **"Registration checked by Open Therapy"**.

<Note>
  Because of this, the registration number field isn't compulsory for the no-board case. Everywhere
  else it is, and it needs at least 6 characters.
</Note>

## The states

Your application moves through five, and the workspace names the one you're in.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Started and not submitted. Nothing is with us yet |
| **Pending** | Submitted and with us. The card reads *"Pending verification"* |
| **Verified** | Checked and published. The card reads *"Verified"* |
| **Needs info** | We couldn't confirm something. The card reads *"Waiting on you"* |
| **Rejected** | We aren't able to publish the profile |

A sixth thing can hold up a published profile without being about you at all: if your care category hasn't opened yet, the card reads *"Waiting on launch"* and says so by name, because "pending verification" would read as a doubt about your registration when the wait is ours. See [Care types](/care-types).

## Listing states

Your profile is either published or it isn't. There's no partial state where clients see some of you.

## If something doesn't line up

You'll get an email — *"We need one more thing."* — saying we couldn't confirm your registration as submitted.

<Note>
  Almost always this is a typo or a name that's since changed, not a problem. If we've left a note
  about what's missing, it appears at the top of your application screen.
</Note>

Fix it from **Edit my answers** on that screen, or reply to the email and a person will pick it up. There's nothing to resubmit from scratch.

If we can't publish a profile at all, we say so plainly rather than leaving it pending, and you can reply and ask us to look again.

You'll get a **"You're live."** email. Three things are worth doing in your first week:

## The verified badge

A verified badge on your profile, and a line in the booking panel: **"Registration checked by Open Therapy"**.

It tells a client we've confirmed you're registered to practise. It is not a rating, a ranking signal, or an endorsement of clinical judgement.

Your **Qualifications** section shows your registering body, your years practising and any qualifications on your profile.

## Keeping your registration current

Your obligations are your own: current registration with Ahpra or your professional association, appropriate professional indemnity insurance, and your own record-keeping, consent and risk processes. Those are terms of listing.

<Note>
  There's no field in the workspace for your insurance certificate or a Working with Children Check, so
  nothing here tracks their expiry. Holding them is on you. An expired registration is grounds for
  unpublishing a profile.
</Note>

## Concerns raised about you

A concern about conduct or clinical care goes through the support form at [opentherapy.app/support](https://opentherapy.app/support), or to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app). Pick the Clinical Concern topic and it's answered within one business day.

Clients can also raise a matter directly with Ahpra, independently of Open Therapy. See [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy) and [Getting help](/getting-help).
