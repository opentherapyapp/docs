Every profile on Open Therapy has had its practitioner's registration checked before going live. Applications that don't clear are not published.

## What's checked

Your registration number against the register held by the body you nominated.

| Body | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Ahpra** | Psychology, psychiatry, occupational therapy, and other registered health professions |
| **Speech Pathology Australia** | Speech pathologists |
| **Dietitians Australia** | Dietitians |
| **PACFA** or **ACA** | Counsellors and psychotherapists |

## Listing states

Your profile is either published or it isn't. There's no partial state where clients see some of you.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Unpublished" icon="eye-off">
    Invisible everywhere — the directory, search, matching and your booking link. You can still work on
    your profile in the workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Published" icon="globe">
    Live in the directory, eligible for matching, and bookable if your payouts are set up.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## If something doesn't line up

You'll get an email — *"One thing to sort out on your application"* — saying we couldn't confirm your registration as submitted.

<Note>
  Almost always this is a typo or a name that's since changed, not a problem. Update your details from
  the link in the email and it goes back into the queue.
</Note>

If you think we've got it wrong, reply to that email and a person will take another look.

## When you're verified

You'll get a **"You're live on Open Therapy"** email. Three things are worth doing in your first week:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your real availability">
    So the times clients see are the times you have. See [Availability](/therapists/availability).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a short reel">
    Profiles with one get roughly twice the enquiries. See
    [Build your profile](/therapists/build-your-profile#media).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your fees and rebates">
    Make sure what's listed is what you charge and what you accept. See
    [Services and pricing](/therapists/services-and-pricing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The verified badge

A verified badge on your profile, and the line **"Registration checked by Open Therapy"** in the booking panel.

It tells a client that we've confirmed you're registered to practise. It is not a rating, a ranking signal, or an endorsement of clinical judgement.

## Keeping registration current

Your profile shows your registration and its expiry. Keep it current — an expired registration is grounds for unpublishing a profile.

Your indemnity insurance and Working with Children Check are recorded against your listing in the same place.

## Concerns raised about you

Conduct and clinical care concerns go to [governance@opentherapy.app](mailto:governance@opentherapy.app) and are acknowledged the same business day. Clients can also raise matters directly with Ahpra, independently of Open Therapy — see [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy#reporting-a-concern).
