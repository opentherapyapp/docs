Every practitioner is checked before their profile goes live. The directory only shows a profile that is both published and verified.

## What's checked

The number you gave, against the register or membership roll held by the body you nominated.

| Body | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Ahpra** | Psychology, psychiatry, occupational therapy, physiotherapy, osteopathy, chiropractic, podiatry |
| **Speech Pathology Australia** | Speech pathology |
| **Dietitians Australia** | Dietetics and nutrition |
| **PACFA** or **ACA** | Counselling and psychotherapy |
| **ESSA** | Exercise physiology |
| **ANZACATA** | Art therapy |
| **AMTA** | Music therapy |
| **Something else, or none** | Support work and developmental education |

- **Ahpra is the register, where there is one.** Registration under the National Law is public record, which we check directly.
- **Speech pathology and counselling are not registered professions.** We check membership of the professional association instead.
- **Some roles have no board at all.** A support worker or developmental educator is verified on a worker screening check, and their profile reads **"Worker screening checked by Open Therapy"** rather than **"Registration checked by Open Therapy"**.

<Note>
  The registration number isn't compulsory for the no-board case. Everywhere else it is, and it needs
  at least 6 characters.
</Note>

## The states

The workspace names the state you're in.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Started and not submitted. Nothing is with us yet |
| **Pending** | Submitted and with us. The card reads *"Pending verification"* |
| **Verified** | Checked and published. The card reads *"Verified"* |
| **Needs info** | We couldn't confirm something. The card reads *"Waiting on you"* |
| **Rejected** | We aren't able to publish the profile |

A published profile can also wait on its care category: the card then reads *"Waiting on launch"* and names the category. See [Care types](/care-types).

## If something doesn't line up

You'll get an email — *"We need one more thing."* — saying we couldn't confirm your registration.

<Note>
  Almost always this is a typo or a name that's since changed. Any note about what's missing appears at
  the top of your application screen.
</Note>

Fix it from **Edit my answers**, or reply to the email — there's nothing to resubmit from scratch. If we can't publish a profile at all, we say so, and you can ask us to look again.

## When you're verified

You'll get a **"You're live."** email. Three things are worth doing in your first week:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hand out your booking link">
    It goes live the same day, a client who books through it never costs you an introduction fee, and a
    click counts for 90 days. See [Your booking link](/therapists/your-booking-link).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your real availability">
    So the times clients see are the times you have. See [Availability](/therapists/availability).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your fees and funding">
    See [Services and pricing](/therapists/services-and-pricing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

A short reel takes thirty seconds. See [Build your profile](/therapists/build-your-profile#photo-and-reel).

## The verified badge

A verified badge on your profile, and a line in the booking panel: **"Registration checked by Open Therapy"**. It confirms you're registered to practise, and is not a rating, a ranking signal, or an endorsement of clinical judgement.

Your **Qualifications** section shows your registering body, your years practising and any qualifications on your profile.

## Keeping your registration current

Your obligations are terms of listing: current registration with Ahpra or your professional association, appropriate professional indemnity insurance, and your own record-keeping, consent and risk processes.

<Note>
  Nothing here tracks your insurance certificate or a Working with Children Check — there's no field
  for either. An expired registration is grounds for unpublishing a profile.
</Note>

## Concerns raised about you

A concern about conduct or clinical care goes through the support form at [opentherapy.app/support](https://opentherapy.app/support), or to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app). Pick the Clinical Concern topic and it's answered within one business day.

Clients can also raise a matter directly with Ahpra, independently of Open Therapy. See [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy) and [Getting help](/getting-help).
