Every therapist gets a personal address at **`opentherapy.link/your-name`**. It opens your profile, exactly as it appears in the directory.

## Why it matters commercially

**A client who arrives through your own link carries no introduction fee.** There is no platform fee and no monthly fee either, so card processing at Stripe's actual cost is the only deduction on that session.

Worth putting on your card, in your email signature and on your own website.

<Note>
  Attribution is first touch. The handle is remembered for 90 days, and the pairing is written the
  moment the client signs in, or at checkout at the latest. Somebody who followed one therapist's link
  and then another's belongs to the first.
</Note>

The pairing is per therapist. If the client later books someone else from the directory, that therapist's introduction fee applies as normal.

## Claiming your handle

Your handle is the second question in the practice setup, and it's held for you the moment you claim it. Afterwards you'll find it under **Your link** in **Settings**, where availability is checked as you type.

The rules:

- Between **3 and 30 characters**
- Lowercase letters, numbers and single hyphens
- No double hyphens, and it can't start or end with one

Anything else you type is folded into that shape rather than rejected — capitals are lowercased, spaces and punctuation become hyphens.

## If your handle isn't available

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| *"Use 3 to 30 letters, numbers and single hyphens."* | The format is wrong |
| *"That one is reserved. Try adding your discipline or suburb."* | It clashes with a page on the site — like `about`, `blog`, `browse` or `fees` |
| *"Someone already has that one."* | Another therapist holds it |
| *"That one belonged to another therapist. Pick a different name."* | It's retired. Retired names are never reissued |

Where a suffixed version would do, we offer one — **Try sarah-chen-2** — for you to accept.

<Tip>
  If your name is taken, add your discipline or your suburb: `sarah-chen-psychology` or
  `sarah-chen-newtown`.
</Tip>

## Before you're verified

The address is reserved from the day you claim it, and the page behind it opens the day your registration is verified. Until then the panel reads **Your link, reserved** and the page returns nothing.

<Warning>
  Don't hand it out before you're live. The first client who taps a reserved link gets a dead page.
</Warning>

## Changing it later

<Warning>
  You can change your handle once every **30 days**. If you try sooner, we tell you how many days are
  left.
</Warning>

Your profile moves with it, and your old link keeps working: the previous name is retired to you and redirects to the current one. It never resolves to anyone else, and you can always take your own former name back.

## What clients see

Your profile, at your address. Nothing on it says a client came in more cheaply than another one.

## Using it well

The workspace gives you two things to copy: the link itself, and a line for your email signature — *"Book a session with me: https://opentherapy.link/your-name"*.
