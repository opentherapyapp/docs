Open Therapy pays you through Stripe. You need a connected account before clients can book you. Set it up under **Payouts** in your workspace.

## You are the merchant of record

Every payment is made on your behalf. Your name — not Open Therapy's — appears on the client's card statement, the charge settles in your country, and the interchange is priced as yours.

Onboarding asks for full verification, and an unverified account can't take a booking. The charge lands on Open Therapy's balance, and your share leaves later as an explicit transfer.

## Setting up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start onboarding">
    Select **Set up payouts with Stripe**. Have your ID and ABN with you — it takes about ten minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give Stripe your details">
    Stripe verifies your identity, your ABN and your bank account. Open Therapy stores a reference to
    your Stripe account and nothing else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back">
    Stripe returns you to Open Therapy, showing **Checking** while it asks Stripe directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If Stripe still needs something, the page lists it in your words: *"A photo of your ID"*, *"Your ABN"*, *"Your bank account details"*, *"Accepting Stripe's terms"*. Anything unusual is shown in Stripe's own term.

## Account states

| State | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Not set up** | No account yet | *"Clients can't book you until Stripe has verified your identity, ABN and bank account."* Start onboarding |
| **Unfinished** | You started with Stripe and didn't finish | **Continue with Stripe** picks up where you left off |
| **Action needed** | Stripe needs something more before you can take bookings | The outstanding items are listed. Resolve them and come back |
| **Active** | Fully verified | Take bookings and receive transfers |

  you do, nobody can book and pay you."*
</Warning>

## Changing your bank details

Select **Change bank details** to open your Stripe dashboard. Bank details and your payout schedule live with Stripe, not with Open Therapy.

## When you get paid

<Steps>
  <Step title="The client pays at booking">
    Your full session fee is taken at checkout and held.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement — when the session is closed off">
    Recording attendance divides the session into its fees and writes your net. Cancellations settle
    against the cancellation fee rather than the price of the hour. See
    [Managing sessions](/therapists/managing-sessions#recording-attendance).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer — on the next hourly pass">
    A scheduled pass runs every hour, on the hour, and moves each settled net to your Stripe balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Stripe then pays your balance to your bank on the schedule you set in your Stripe dashboard. Most banks show it within one to two business days.

<Note>
  Nothing settles and nothing transfers until the session is closed off. That's the one step that's
  yours.
</Note>

The **How you get paid** panel still says a session is transferred *the day after* you mark it complete. That's out of date: the pass runs hourly, so a session closed off at 10am is usually transferred by 11. The schedule is `payouts:transfer` in `vite.config.ts`.

## Why a transfer might not have happened

Five conditions have to hold before the pass will move a settlement:

| | |
|---|---|
| The settlement is still pending | Not already transferred, reversed or failed |
| The client's payment completed | The charge went through |
| The real card processing fee is known | Stripe's number, not an estimate |
| Transfers are enabled on your account | Stripe's verdict, not ours |
| Your account isn't frozen | See below |

The transfer is keyed on the settlement, so a retry can't pay you twice.

<Note>
  The one to watch is the third. A session whose fee Stripe hasn't stated yet waits rather than being
  paid out on a guess. Stripe is asked again every hour.
</Note>

A failed transfer records Stripe's own words for why, and isn't picked up again by the hourly pass. If a session sits unpaid, write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app) with the session date.

## Frozen accounts

A chargeback on any of your sessions freezes transfers across your whole account while it's open. It's a precaution, not a judgement about the session.

You're emailed: *"Your payouts are paused while this is open."* A dispute that closes in your favour lifts the freeze, and the next transfer includes anything held while it was open. One that goes against you doesn't lift it and needs a person — write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app).

See [Refunds and disputes](/therapists/refunds-and-disputes).

## Payout emails

When money reaches your bank you get a **Payout** email: *"Your payout for August has been sent. Most banks show it within one to two business days."*

That figure is your net earnings; introduction fees were taken at settlement and are invoiced separately. See [Invoices and tax](/therapists/invoices-and-tax).

A payout returned by your bank is emailed to you too, and the money goes back onto your Open Therapy balance. It's almost always the account details — a closed account, a mistyped BSB, or a name the bank won't match. Fix them in your Stripe dashboard and the next payout picks it up along with anything since.
