Open Therapy pays you through Stripe. You need a connected account before clients can book you.

Set it up under **Payouts** in your workspace.

## You are the merchant of record

Every payment is made on your behalf. Your name — not Open Therapy's — appears on the client's card statement, the charge settles in your country, and the interchange is priced as yours.

Stripe only allows that for an account with a full payments capability, which is why onboarding asks for full verification and why an unverified account can't take a booking. The charge still lands on Open Therapy's balance and your share leaves later as an explicit transfer, because that's the only arrangement where the money can be held until the session has actually happened.

## Setting up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start onboarding">
    Select **Set up payouts with Stripe**. You'll be handed to Stripe. Have your ID and ABN with you —
    it takes about ten minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give Stripe your details">
    Stripe collects and verifies your identity, your ABN and the bank account you want to be paid into.
    Open Therapy stores a reference to your Stripe account and nothing else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back">
    Stripe returns you to Open Therapy. The page waits a moment and asks Stripe directly rather than
    showing you a stale answer — it says **Checking** while it does.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If Stripe still needs something, the page lists it in your words rather than Stripe's: *"A photo of your ID"*, *"Your ABN"*, *"Your bank account details"*, *"Accepting Stripe's terms"*. Anything unusual is shown in Stripe's own term, because that's what you'll need if you ring them.

## Account states

| State | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Not set up** | No account yet | *"Clients can't book you until Stripe has verified your identity, ABN and bank account."* Start onboarding |
| **Unfinished** | You started with Stripe and didn't finish | **Continue with Stripe** picks up where you left off |
| **Action needed** | Stripe needs something more before you can take bookings | The outstanding items are listed. Resolve them and come back |
| **Active** | Fully verified | Take bookings and receive transfers |

## Changing your bank details

Select **Change bank details**, which opens your Stripe dashboard. Bank details live with Stripe, not with Open Therapy, and so does your payout schedule.

## When you get paid

Three stages, and it helps to keep them separate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The client pays at booking">
    Your full session fee is taken at checkout and held. Nothing is divided yet, because the split
    depends on what the card actually cost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement — when the session is closed off">
    Recording attendance divides the session into its fees and writes your net. Cancellations settle the
    same way, against the cancellation fee rather than the price of the hour. See
    [Managing sessions](/therapists/managing-sessions#recording-attendance).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer — on the next hourly pass">
    A scheduled pass runs every hour, on the hour, and moves each settled net to your Stripe balance.
    Nothing pays out from a request, and nothing needs a second press from you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Stripe then pays your balance to your bank on the schedule you set in your Stripe dashboard. Most banks show it within one to two business days.

<Note>
  Nothing settles and nothing transfers until the session is closed off. That's the one step that's
  yours.
</Note>

The **How you get paid** panel on that screen still says a session is transferred *the day after* you
mark it complete. That's out of date and reads slower than reality: the pass runs hourly, so a session
closed off at 10am is usually transferred by 11. `payouts:transfer` in `vite.config.ts` is the schedule
that actually governs it.

## Why a transfer might not have happened

Five conditions have to hold before the pass will move a settlement, and it asks all five in one query so no other code path can hold a looser version of them:

| | |
|---|---|
| The settlement is still pending | Not already transferred, reversed or failed |
| The client's payment completed | The charge went through |
| The real card processing fee is known | Stripe's number, not an estimate |
| Transfers are enabled on your account | Stripe's verdict, not ours |
| Your account isn't frozen | See below |

The transfer is held against the specific charge that paid for the session, so it waits for those funds rather than drawing on anyone else's, and it's keyed on the settlement — a retry after a timeout can't pay you twice.

<Note>
  The one to watch is the third. Your share is worked out from the real processing fee, so a session
  whose fee Stripe hasn't stated yet waits rather than being paid out on a guess. It shows on your
  earnings screen under **Waiting on a Stripe fee**, and we ask Stripe again every hour.
</Note>

A transfer that fails records Stripe's own words for why. That isn't picked up again by the hourly pass, so if a session sits unpaid with no obvious reason, write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app) with the session date.

## Frozen accounts

A chargeback on any of your sessions freezes transfers across your whole account while it's open. That's a precaution, not a judgement about the session — Open Therapy carries the loss on a disputed payment, so freezing is the cheap and reversible move.

You're emailed when it happens, and told plainly: *"Your payouts are paused while this is open."* A dispute that closes in your favour lifts the freeze and the next transfer includes anything held while it was open. One that goes against you doesn't lift it, and needs a person — write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app).

See [Refunds and disputes](/therapists/refunds-and-disputes).

## Payout emails

When money reaches your bank you get a **Payout** email: *"Your payout for August has been sent. Most banks show it within one to two business days."*

That figure is your net earnings. Introduction fees and card processing were taken at settlement and are invoiced separately rather than netted invisibly, so your income and your costs stay legible at tax time. See [Invoices and tax](/therapists/invoices-and-tax).

If a payout is returned by your bank you're told that too, and the money goes back onto your Open Therapy balance rather than being lost. It's almost always the account details — a closed account, a mistyped BSB, or a name the bank won't match. Fix them in your Stripe dashboard and the next scheduled payout picks it up along with anything since.
