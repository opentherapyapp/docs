## Apply

Start from the **For therapists** page on the public site.

<Frame caption="Click **List your practice — free** to begin">
  <img src="/images/for-therapists.webp" width="2880" height="1800" style={{ width: 1440 }} alt="The public For therapists page headed Free to list. Free to grow., with the line one fee, once, per new client we match you with, no subscription, no commission, and buttons reading List your practice — free and See what it costs" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up as a therapist">
    Choose the therapist option, with the email address you want us to reach you on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    Enter the six-digit code, valid for 30 minutes, then choose a password of at least 8 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your practice">
    You're taken straight into it. Set aside about fifteen minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The setup saves as you go, so you can leave a screen half-answered and come back to it.

## The five phases

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Registration" icon="badge-check" defaultOpen>
    Your care category, then the title you're registered under where the category holds more than one
    — Psychology has two, Counselling has one. Then your registering body, registration number and
    years practising.

    **To continue:** a category and a title, a body, and a registration number of at least 6 characters.
    Support and developmental education roles aren't asked for a number. See
    [Verification](/therapists/verification).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your practice" icon="map-pin">
    **Online**, **In person** or **Home visit**, your timezone, locations and languages. Then the rough
    shape of your week: which days, and mornings, afternoons or evenings after 6pm. Exact hours come
    later, on [Availability](/therapists/availability).

    **To continue:** at least one session mode, a timezone, and a usable address if you see people in
    person.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your work" icon="list">
    What you work with, your approaches and modalities, then the lived-experience themes. Each of the
    eight themes you pick adds a screen where you mark experiences as **I've lived this** or **I work
    with it**, plus the lived part in your own words, up to 600 characters. These markings drive
    matching — see
    [Build your profile](/therapists/build-your-profile#lived-experience-and-what-you-work-with).

    **To continue:** at least one thing you work with.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fees" icon="dollar-sign">
    The funding you accept — Medicare rebates, NDIS, private health extras, DVA — then your services: a
    name, a length, a price and an optional description for each. Up to eight.

    **To continue:** one complete service, named, timed and priced. See
    [Services and pricing](/therapists/services-and-pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your profile" icon="user">
    Your name as you practise under it, your pronouns, a photo and your bio. Then your booking address
    on `opentherapy.link`, claimed on the way out of the screen. Then an optional thirty-second intro
    reel.

    **To continue:** a first and last name, and a bio of at least 40 characters. See
    [Build your profile](/therapists/build-your-profile) and
    [Your booking link](/therapists/your-booking-link).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Have your registration number to hand — it's the field most likely to send your application back.
</Tip>

## What happens next

Submitting sets your application to **pending**, and your profile publishes when your registration clears. Nothing is visible to clients before then — not the directory, not matching, not your booking link. See [Verification](/therapists/verification).

<Note>
  **Edit my answers** reopens the setup after submitting, with everything you entered still in it.
  Changing your details doesn't cost you your place.
</Note>

## Before a client can book you

Verification publishes your profile. Three more things decide whether anyone can book.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Set up payouts" icon="banknote" href="/therapists/set-up-payouts" horizontal>
    Clients can't check out until Stripe has verified your identity, ABN and bank account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Publish your hours" icon="calendar" href="/therapists/availability" horizontal>
    A profile with no published hours can't be booked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Choose a cancellation policy" icon="calendar-x" href="/therapists/cancellation-policy" horizontal>
    A session booked before you choose one carries no cancellation fee at all.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Check your care type is open" icon="clock" href="/care-types" horizontal>
    Five care types are bookable. The other eleven are still to open. See [Care types](/care-types).
  </Card>
</Columns>

Unfinished payouts, no published hours and unanswered messages each appear under **Needs you** on your workspace **Overview**.
