## Apply

You start from the **For therapists** page on the public site.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up as a therapist">
    On the signup page choose the therapist option. Use the email address you want us to reach you on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    We send a six-digit code, valid for 30 minutes. Enter it, then choose a password of at least 8
    characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your practice">
    You're taken straight into it. Set aside about fifteen minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The setup lives at **Set up your practice** and it saves as you go, so you can leave a screen half-answered and come back to it.

## The five phases

The wizard draws a rail across the top with a segment per phase. There are more screens than phases — one for each experience theme you pick, for instance — so the rail names where you are rather than counting to a number.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Registration" icon="badge-check" defaultOpen>
    Your care category first — *"What kind of care do you offer?"* — and then, where the category holds
    more than one title, which one you're registered under. Psychology has two, Counselling has one.

    Then your registering body, your registration number and your years practising.

    **To continue:** a category and a title, a body, and a registration number of at least 6 characters.
    Support and developmental education roles have no board and no number, so the number isn't asked of
    them. See [Verification](/therapists/verification).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your practice" icon="map-pin">
    How you work — **Online**, **In person**, **Home visit** — your timezone, your locations, and the
    languages you practise in.

    Then a rough shape of your week: which days, and mornings, afternoons or evenings after 6pm. Exact
    hours come later in your workspace, on [Availability](/therapists/availability).

    **To continue:** at least one session mode, a timezone, and — only if you've said you see people in
    person — a usable address.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your work" icon="list">
    What you work with, your approaches and modalities, and then the lived-experience themes.

    You pick which of the eight themes you hold, and each one you pick adds a short screen where you
    mark the specific experiences inside it as **I've lived this** or **I work with it**. Leave the rest
    blank. Last comes the option to say the lived part in your own words, up to 600 characters.

    This is the part that drives matching, and it's worth the time. See
    [Build your profile](/therapists/build-your-profile#lived-experience-and-what-you-work-with).

    **To continue:** at least one thing you work with.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fees" icon="dollar-sign">
    The funding you accept — Medicare rebates, NDIS, private health extras, DVA — and then your
    services: a name, a length, a price and an optional description for each. Up to eight.

    **To continue:** one complete service, named, timed and priced.

    See [Services and pricing](/therapists/services-and-pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your profile" icon="user">
    The card a client reads: your name as you practise under it, your pronouns, a photo and your bio.
    Then your booking address on `opentherapy.link`, which is claimed on the way out of the screen and
    held for you. Then, optionally, a thirty-second intro reel — *"Filmed on a phone is fine."*

    **To continue:** a first and last name, and a bio of at least 40 characters. The photo and the reel
    are genuinely optional.

    See [Build your profile](/therapists/build-your-profile) and
    [Your booking link](/therapists/your-booking-link).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Have your registration number to hand before you start. It's the one field you can't guess at, and
  the one most likely to send your application back for a correction.
</Tip>

## What happens next

Submitting sets your application to **pending**. We check your registration with the body you nominated, and your profile publishes when that clears.

Nothing is visible to clients before then — not the directory, not matching, not your booking link. See [Verification](/therapists/verification) for the states and what happens if something doesn't line up.

<Note>
  You can reopen the setup after submitting. The completed screen carries **Edit my answers**, which
  puts you back into the flow with everything you entered still in it. Changing your details doesn't
  cost you your place.
</Note>

## Before a client can book you

Verification publishes your profile. Three more things decide whether anyone can actually take an hour.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Set up payouts" icon="banknote" href="/therapists/set-up-payouts" horizontal>
    Clients can't check out until Stripe has verified your identity, ABN and bank account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Publish your hours" icon="calendar" href="/therapists/availability" horizontal>
    A profile with no hours published can't be booked, whatever else is right.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Choose a cancellation policy" icon="calendar-x" href="/therapists/cancellation-policy" horizontal>
    A session booked before you choose one carries no cancellation fee at all.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Check your care type is open" icon="clock" href="/care-types" horizontal>
    Psychology and Counselling are bookable. The other twelve categories are still to open.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Your workspace **Overview** keeps this honest: unfinished payouts, no published hours and unanswered messages each appear under **Needs you** until they're dealt with.
