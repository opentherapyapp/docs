## What comes off a session

You keep the session fee, less the following.

| Deduction | Amount |
|---|---|
| Introduction fee | **\$70–\$120**, once per new client, by discipline |
| Card processing | Stripe's actual cost, passed through |
| GST | **10%** on Open Therapy's own fee |

There is **no platform fee**. It was a percentage of every session and the rate is now zero. It appears only on statements settled while it applied.

Nothing is ever added to what the client pays. The full table of introduction fees by discipline is on [Fees](/fees), the canonical page.

<Note>
  The session itself is GST-free health care. GST applies to Open Therapy's fee to you, a taxable
  supply — claimable if you're registered. Card processing arrives GST-inclusive, so its tax is
  carved back out rather than added again.
</Note>

## The shape of it

Card processing is Stripe's own number, so it varies with the card. The figures below use the published Australian domestic rate — 1.7% plus 30c — as an estimate: \$180 quotes at \$3.36 and \$220 at \$4.04.

### What that averages to

The introduction fee is charged once, so your effective rate falls with every session the same client books. Six \$180 sessions with one new client, at the psychologist's \$90:

- Gross: 6 × \$180.00 = \$1,080.00
- First session deductions: \$90.00 + \$9.00 + \$3.36 = \$102.36
- The other five: 5 × \$3.36 = \$16.80
- Total deducted: \$119.16
- You receive: \$960.84

\$119.16 of \$1,080.00 is **11.0%** across those six sessions. Over twelve sessions it's \$102.36 plus eleven lots of \$3.36, so \$139.32 of \$2,160.00, or **6.5%**. There is no single blended figure.

## Group programs

A group enrolment is charged a flat **15% commission** plus GST on the commission, and nothing else. No introduction fee, and no card processing passed through.

A \$400.00 enrolment:

| | |
|---|---|
| Client pays | \$400.00 |
| Commission at 15% | −\$60.00 |
| GST on the commission | −\$6.00 |
| **You receive** | **\$334.00** |

\$66.00 deducted, or 16.5%, whichever card the client used. See [Groups](/therapists/groups).

## The two clamps

Both exist so a session can never cost you money.

### A payout can never go negative

An introduction fee larger than the session it lands on is reduced to what the session can carry after card processing and GST. You earn nothing on that session rather than owing us the difference.

Take a psychologist's \$90 introduction fee against a \$90.00 session — a first appointment cancelled late on Standard, where the \$90.00 cancellation fee becomes the gross:

- Room for our fees, ex-GST: \$90.00 − \$3.36 = \$86.64, and \$86.64 ÷ 1.1 = \$78.76
- The introduction fee is charged at \$78.76 rather than \$90.00
- GST on it: \$7.88
- You receive: \$90.00 − \$78.76 − \$3.36 − \$7.88 = **\$0.00**

Zero, not a debt. The \$11.24 of introduction fee that didn't fit is not carried forward.

### The processing fee is clamped too

Stripe keeps its fee when money goes back to a client, and that cost was taken against the full price. On a partial refund it's clamped the same way and you net zero.

### Earnings by month

Twelve months of **net** earnings, oldest first, with the current month flagged. Months with no work show as empty. A session counts in the month it *settled*, not the month it happened.

### This month

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Billed** | What clients paid |
| **New client introductions** | Introduction fees charged in the period |
| **Card processing** | What the cards actually cost |
| **GST on our fees** | Tax on Open Therapy's fee |
| **Yours** | What reached you |

<Note>
  A retired platform fee only appears on a period old enough to have paid one. It isn't printed as a
  \$0.00 row forever.
</Note>

### Sessions

One line per session — *"each fee as it was charged on the day"* — with **Billed**, **Platform**, **Introduction**, **Card**, **GST** and **Yours**. A fee that wasn't charged shows an em dash rather than a zero.

### Payouts

Each payout with its amount, period and date, badged **Paid** or **Processing**. Empty until a settled session has transferred.

### Our invoices to you

Your monthly fee invoices, with the total, the period and the GST inside it. They're issued **Paid**. See [Invoices and tax](/therapists/invoices-and-tax).

### Waiting on you, and waiting on Stripe

Both panels appear only when they have something in them. **Waiting on you** is sessions you haven't recorded attendance for — nothing settles or transfers until you do. **Waiting on a Stripe fee** is closed sessions whose real processing fee hasn't arrived. See [Managing sessions](/therapists/managing-sessions#recording-attendance).

If your payout setup isn't finished, a banner sits above the numbers: clients can't check out at all.

## Fee changes never re-price the past

Every settlement stores the rates that applied when it was made, and the invoice recomputes its lines from those frozen rows. A change applies to new sessions only.

## Reconciling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from the payout">
    A payout is the sum of the settlements transferred in that period, already net of everything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match it against the sessions table">
    Each line's **Yours** column is what went into a payout, in the month it settled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take your expenses from the invoice, not the payout">
    The fee invoice states the fees ex-GST with the GST separated. See
    [Invoices and tax](/therapists/invoices-and-tax).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Don't reconstruct your fees by subtracting net from gross. That total mixes a deductible expense, a
  GST credit and a cost recharged at cost.
</Tip>

Open Therapy can't advise you on tax. Your accountant can, and the fee invoice is the document to hand them.
