## What comes off a session

You keep the published session price, less the following.

| Deduction | Amount |
|---|---|
| Introduction fee | **\$70–\$120**, once per new client, by discipline |
| GST | **10%** on Open Therapy's own fee |

There is **no platform fee**. It was a percentage of every session and the rate is now zero. It appears only on statements settled while it applied.

**Card processing is added at checkout**, so the client pays it and you keep the published fee. The full table of introduction fees by discipline is on [Fees](/fees), the canonical page.

<Note>
  The session itself is GST-free health care. GST applies to Open Therapy's fee to you, a taxable
  supply — claimable if you're registered. On older settlements that recharged card processing to
  you, that line arrived GST-inclusive, so its tax was carved back out rather than added again.
</Note>

## The shape of it

Card processing is quoted to the client at Stripe's published Australian domestic rate — 1.7% plus 30c — covering the whole charge: \$180 quotes at **\$3.42** (client pays **\$183.42**) and \$220 at **\$4.11** (client pays **\$224.11**). That amount is not taken from you.

### What that averages to

The introduction fee is charged once, so your effective rate falls with every session the same client books. Six \$180 sessions with one new client, at the psychologist's \$90:

- Session fees billed: 6 × \$180.00 = \$1,080.00
- First session deductions: \$90.00 + \$9.00 = \$99.00
- The other five: nothing
- Total deducted: \$99.00
- You receive: \$981.00

\$99.00 of \$1,080.00 is **9.2%** across those six sessions. Over twelve sessions it's still \$99.00 of \$2,160.00, or **4.6%**. There is no single blended figure.

## Group programs

<Info>
  Group programs are being rolled out. The commission below is what applies once a program can take
  enrolments.
</Info>

A group enrolment is charged a flat **15% commission** plus GST on the commission, and nothing else. No introduction fee, and no card processing passed through.

A \$400.00 enrolment:

| | |
|---|---|
| Client pays | \$400.00 |
| Commission at 15% | −\$60.00 |
| GST on the commission | −\$6.00 |
| **You receive** | **\$334.00** |

\$66.00 deducted, or 16.5%, whichever card the client used. See [Groups](/therapists/groups).

## The clamp

A payout can never go negative. An introduction fee larger than the session it lands on is reduced to what the session can carry after GST. You earn next to nothing on that session rather than owing us the difference.

Take a psychologist's \$90 introduction fee against a \$90.00 session — a first appointment cancelled late on Standard, where the \$90.00 cancellation fee becomes the gross:

- Room for our fees, ex-GST: \$90.00 ÷ 1.1 = \$81.81
- The introduction fee is charged at \$81.81 rather than \$90.00
- GST on it: \$8.18
- You receive: \$90.00 − \$81.81 − \$8.18 = **\$0.01**

A cent, not a debt. The \$8.19 of introduction fee that didn't fit is not carried forward.

### Earnings by month

Twelve months of **net** earnings, oldest first, with the current month flagged. Months with no work show as empty. A session counts in the month it *settled*, not the month it happened.

### This month

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Billed** | Session fees charged |
| **Platform fees** | Introduction fees charged in the period |
| **GST on platform fees** | Tax on Open Therapy's fee |
| **Yours** | What reached you |

<Note>
  A retired platform fee only appears on a period old enough to have paid one. Card processing
  only appears if an older settlement still deducted it. Neither is printed as a \$0.00 row
  forever.
</Note>

### Sessions

One line per session — *"each fee as it was charged on the day"* — with **Billed**, **Introduction**, **GST** and **Yours**. **Platform** and **Card** columns appear only when a row in the period actually carried that fee. A fee that wasn't charged shows an em dash rather than a zero.

Tabs on the same screen: **Sessions**, **Invoices**, **Payments**, and **Platform fees** for the monthly invoices Open Therapy raises to you.

### Payouts

Each payout with its amount, period and date, badged **Paid** or **Processing**. Empty until a settled session has transferred. Bank payouts also appear on [Payouts](/therapists/set-up-payouts).

### Our invoices to you

Under the **Platform fees** tab: your monthly fee invoices, with the total, the period and the GST inside it. They're issued **Paid**. See [Invoices and tax](/therapists/invoices-and-tax).

### Not closed yet

This panel appears only when it has something in it: sessions you haven't recorded attendance for. A session closes on its own a day after it ends; a no-show has to be recorded before then. See [Managing sessions](/therapists/managing-sessions#recording-attendance).

If your payout setup isn't finished, a banner sits above the numbers: clients can't check out at all.

## Fee changes never re-price the past

Every settlement stores the rates that applied when it was made, and the invoice recomputes its lines from those frozen rows. A change applies to new sessions only.

## Reconciling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from the payout">
    A payout is the sum of the settlements transferred in that period, already net of everything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match it against the sessions table">
    Each line's **Yours** column is what went into a payout, in the month it settled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take your expenses from the invoice, not the payout">
    The fee invoice states the fees ex-GST with the GST separated. See
    [Invoices and tax](/therapists/invoices-and-tax).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Don't reconstruct your fees by subtracting net from gross. That total mixes a deductible expense
  and a GST credit.
</Tip>

Open Therapy can't advise you on tax. Your accountant can, and the fee invoice is the document to hand them.
