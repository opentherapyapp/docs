You set your own prices. Open Therapy never changes them and never adds anything to what the client pays.

Your services live under **Fees** in the workspace. Edits go live immediately. **Edit my answers** in setup also reopens the same questions.

## Meet and greet

A short, free online call for someone deciding whether to book. It is on by default. Turn it off, or pick **5 min**, **10 min** or **15 min**, on the same **Fees** screen.

It sits inside an hour you have already published — several can run back to back in that one hour. A meet and greet is always online and always instant, even if your paid services are request-to-book. Someone books it without a card.

<Note>
  Without online among your session modes there is nothing to offer, and the switch says so rather
  than sitting dead.
</Note>

## Defining a service

Each service has its own length and price, and you can list up to eight.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | What you call it, up to 80 characters |
| **Length** | 30, 45, 50, 60, 80, 90 or 120 minutes. An unusual length you already use is kept |
| **Fee** | What the client pays |
| **Description** | What happens in it and who it's for, up to 240 characters |

<Note>
  The price and length are applied from what you've set when the booking is priced. The browser can't
  supply an amount. Listed and sliding-scale prices must be at least the introduction fee for your
  discipline, so a first session can carry it.
</Note>

### What kind of session it is

An optional **What kind of session?** question sits under each service, with a short list of chips for your discipline. It decides which service page your session is listed on, alongside everyone else who offers it.

Tagging means a client searching for *ADHD screening* finds your *ADHD assessment*, and you keep your own wording.

- If you don't answer, we read a tag from the name and say so. Change it and your answer sticks.
- If nothing fits, press **None of these fits** and tell us what you'd call it. It's on your profile and in search either way.

### What clients see in search

Search results show **from \$X**, taken from your cheapest service, with its length. Clients can find you by any session on the list.

## Directory price filters

A band only ever matches its own unit. The session bands are **under \$130**, **\$130 – \$170**, **\$170 – \$220** and **\$220+**.

Every session counts, not one nominated as the headline — a therapist whose ongoing hour is \$120 answers a filter of under \$130 even if their assessment is \$600.

## Your sliding scale

Optional and published: a lower fee for clients on a low income.

Set up to three bands, each with a household income ceiling and the fee at or under it. Each service then gets a toggle: **Offer this from \$90 on my sliding scale**. Your profile reads as a ladder — *"\$90 under \$40,000, \$130 under \$70,000"*.

<Note>
  Clients tell you which band applies to them when they book, and nothing is checked. A scale also
  makes you findable under the **Sliding scale** funding filter.
</Note>

## Initial consultations

Most practitioners set a separate initial consultation, usually longer and usually higher. The first session with a new client is where the introduction fee lands. See [Fees and earnings](/therapists/fees-and-earnings).

## Funding you accept

Four chips: **Medicare rebates**, **NDIS**, **Private health extras** and **DVA**. They appear on your profile under **Rebates and funding**, as a tick or a dash with a line of detail, and they're a directory filter.

<Warning>
  Only privately paid sessions can be booked through Open Therapy. Bulk-billed, NDIS-invoiced, DVA and
  WorkCover sessions are rejected. Clients pay in full at booking and claim their rebate themselves:
  *"Pay in full at booking. We email an itemised receipt to claim with."*
</Warning>

### Medicare item numbers

Say you accept Medicare rebates and each service gains an optional **Medicare item** field. Pick the item you bill rather than typing an amount — we look up the current benefit, so the figure survives the July indexation.

The choices are filtered by your discipline and by the length above, so a 50-minute psychology appointment offers only the items it could be. If we don't hold what you bill, choose **Another item number…** and enter the item and the rebate yourself.

<Note>
  If you keep an item that no longer suits the length, we say so rather than dropping it.
</Note>

## Shift-based support

Support work is priced by the hour across seven rate bands — weekday daytime, weekday evening, weekday night, Saturday, Sunday, public holiday and sleepover — with its own directory filters of **Under \$80 / hour** and **\$80+ / hour**. Clients engage you through a request rather than a booking, and you invoice after each shift.

<Info>
  Shift pricing isn't part of the practice setup yet, so rate bands and rosters can't be entered there.
  If you work shifts, get in touch at [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app).
</Info>

## Changing your prices

Change them whenever you like. New prices apply to new bookings, and a session already booked keeps the price it was booked at.
