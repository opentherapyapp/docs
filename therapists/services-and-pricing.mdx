You set your own prices. Open Therapy never changes them, and never adds anything to what the client pays.

## Defining a service

Each service has four things:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | What you call it |
| **Duration** | In minutes |
| **Price** | What the client pays |
| **Description** | A line explaining what it is |

Add and edit them under **Services and fees** in **Settings**.

<Note>
  A client picks a service before picking a time, and the price and duration are applied server-side
  from what you've set. The figure they see at checkout is always the figure you configured — the
  browser can't supply an amount.
</Note>

## Service types and search

Practices name the same thing differently — *ADHD screening* here, *ADHD assessment* there. Behind each service you can set a **type**, which is what the directory actually groups and filters on.

This means a client searching *ADHD assessment* finds your *ADHD screening*, and you keep your own wording.

Generic entries like *Initial consultation* or *Review session* deliberately carry no type, because they don't describe a particular piece of work.

## Session fees versus hourly rates

Two delivery models, and they never mix in a price filter.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Appointment-based">
    A fee per session, with a stated length — "\$180 per 50 min". This is how psychology, counselling,
    speech pathology and most allied health work is priced.

    Directory price bands: under \$130, \$130–\$170, \$170–\$220, \$220+.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Shift-based">
    An hourly rate with a minimum block, for disability support work. Clients engage you through a
    request and a service agreement rather than a booking, and you invoice after each shift.

    Directory price bands: under \$80 per hour, \$80+ per hour.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A support worker's four-hour minimum block never gets swept into a per-session price band.

## Shift rate bands

If you work shifts, seven rate bands are available:

| Band | When |
|---|---|
| Weekday day | 6am to 8pm |
| Weekday evening | After 8pm |
| Weekday night | Midnight to 6am |
| Saturday | All day |
| Sunday | All day |
| Public holiday | All day |
| Sleepover | Inactive overnight, per night |

Each offering carries a **minimum hours** figure — two hours is the NDIS norm — plus your weekly roster, how much notice you need, and whether you do overnights.

Directory price filters compare only your weekday-daytime hourly rate, so that's the number clients see on your card.

## Initial consultations

Most practitioners set a separate, usually longer and higher, initial consultation fee. Worth doing: the first session is where the introduction fee lands, so a realistic initial fee protects your margin on it.

See the [worked example](/fees#a-worked-example) on the fees page.

## Funding you accept

Mark which funding types you accept — Medicare rebate, NDIS, private health. These appear on your profile as a tick or a dash with a line of detail, and they're a directory filter.

<Warning>
  Only privately paid sessions can currently be booked through Open Therapy. Bulk-billed, NDIS-invoiced,
  DVA and WorkCover sessions can't be booked online — that's deliberate, so they aren't booked and then
  mishandled. Clients pay in full and claim their rebate themselves.
</Warning>

## Changing your prices

Change them whenever you like. New prices apply to new bookings only. A session already booked keeps the price it was booked at, and a settled session keeps the rate that was in force when it settled.

## Membership discounts

Open Therapy+ members get 20% off sessions. That discount comes out of Open Therapy's margin, not your fee. You're paid the same whether or not the client is a member, and you don't opt in. See [Fees](/fees#membership-discounts-dont-come-out-of-your-fee).
