## The three formats

| Format | How it runs |
|---|---|
| **Course** | A fixed number of sessions building on each other, with the same people each week. You join at the start and finish together |
| **Workshop** | One sitting, one topic, in a room or online |
| **Support group** | Ongoing, drop-in and facilitated |

<Note>
  Group therapy is now a **Course** and a webinar is a **Workshop**. Old links still narrow to the right
  place.
</Note>

## Finding one

Search by topic, and by place if you want a room rather than a screen. The filters:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Courses, Workshops, Support groups |
| **Care type** | Only the care types some program in the catalogue actually covers |
| **How it runs** | Online, In person |
| **Price** | Free, Under \$50 a session, \$50 – \$100 a session, \$100+ a session |
| **Funding** | Medicare rebate, NDIS, Private health, Employer (EAP) |
| **Starting** | The months intakes actually start in |
| **Availability** | Places still open |

Sort by **Starting soonest**, **Lowest price** or **Most places left**. There's a map, but it's never the default.

## Cohorts

Programs run in intakes, so **what you book is a cohort, not the program**. Each one shows its start date, its schedule — *"Six Tuesdays from 4 March"* — and either how many places are left or that it's full. Three or fewer places left is flagged.

## Joining

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an intake">
    Pick the dated cohort that suits you. A full one is still selectable — it becomes a waitlist request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your details">
    If you're not signed in, you make an account here with an emailed code. Then an optional phone
    number, and an optional note for the facilitator: access needs, what you're hoping to get out of it,
    or anything you'd rather they knew.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    The whole program, once, at the time you book. A program priced per session is charged for all of its
    sessions together, so the total you see is the total.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your place is held while you're paying and released if you don't finish. Then you get an email confirming the place, with calendar invites for every session, plus the video link if it runs online or the address and arrival notes if it's in a room.

<Note>
  Booking a place also opens messaging with the facilitator. See [Messages](/clients/messages).
</Note>

## Waitlists

If a cohort is full you can join its waitlist. **Nothing is charged.** If a place opens in that intake it's offered to you, and you have **48 hours** to take it.

## Withdrawing

**Withdraw before the first session and you're refunded in full.** Email us. After the first session the place is yours and the program's own terms apply, on the program page.

## What's on a program page

About this program, who it's for, what happens in a session, the dates and intakes, the facilitators, and funding and rebates, with other programs like it underneath. The price sits beside it with the next intake, where it runs, and a way through to one-to-one care.

Co-facilitated programs list every facilitator, each linking to their profile.

## Cost

A group place is usually a fraction of the equivalent individual fee, and what you see is what you pay — Open Therapy adds nothing on top.

Facilitators pay a **15% commission** on group enrolments, in place of the fees a one-to-one session carries. See [Fees](/fees).

## Rebates

Whether a Medicare, NDIS, private health or employer rebate applies depends on the program, and each program page says which. Group programs are not automatically rebatable just because individual sessions with the same practitioner are — see [Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).

## If a group isn't the right shape

If you're struggling and a program's first session is a fortnight away, don't wait on it — book a session, or see [Getting help](/getting-help) for what's available right now, including **Lifeline** on **13 11 14**.
