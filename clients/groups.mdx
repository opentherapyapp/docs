Not everything works better one to one. A group place is usually a fraction of the individual fee, and for some kinds of work the group itself is the point.

Browse them at **Groups** in the main navigation.

## The three formats

The taxonomy is about what you're being asked to commit to, and nothing else. Whether a program is clinical or educational, online or in a room, free or paid is a filter rather than a format.

| Format | How it runs |
|---|---|
| **Course** | A fixed number of sessions building on each other, with the same people each week. You join at the start and finish together, whether that's a skills course or a therapy group. |
| **Workshop** | One sitting, one topic, in a room or online. Nothing to commit to beyond the day. |
| **Support group** | Ongoing, drop-in and facilitated. Come when you need to, and stop when you don't — there's no course to finish. |

<Note>
  Group therapy and webinars used to be their own rows. Group therapy is now a **Course** and a webinar is
  a **Workshop**. Old links still narrow to the right place.
</Note>

## Finding one

Search by topic, and by place if you want a room rather than a screen. The filters:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Courses, Workshops, Support groups |
| **Care type** | Only the care types some program in the catalogue actually covers |
| **How it runs** | Online, In person |
| **Price** | Free, Under \$50 a session, \$50 – \$100 a session, \$100+ a session |
| **Funding** | Medicare rebate, NDIS, Private health, Employer (EAP) |
| **Starting** | The months intakes actually start in |
| **Availability** | Places still open |

Sort by **Starting soonest**, **Lowest price** or **Most places left**.

There's a map, but it's never the default — around half of this catalogue meets online and has nothing to draw, so answering a suburb search with a map would quietly drop every program you could join from anywhere.

## Cohorts

Programs run in intakes rather than continuously, so **what you book is a cohort, not the program**. Each one shows its start date, its schedule — *"Six Tuesdays from 4 March"* — and either how many places are left or that it's full. Three or fewer places left is flagged, because a program with one place in three weeks is a different proposition from the same program with an intake next year.

## Joining

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an intake">
    Pick the dated cohort that suits you. A full one is still selectable — it becomes a waitlist request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your details">
    If you're not signed in, you make an account here with an emailed code. Then a phone number if you
    want to give one, and an optional note for the facilitator: access needs, what you're hoping to get
    out of it, or anything you'd rather they knew in advance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    The whole program, once, at the time you book. A program priced per session is charged for all of its
    sessions together, so the total you see is the total.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your place is held while you're paying and released if you don't finish, so an abandoned checkout doesn't sit on a seat someone else wants.

Then you get an email confirming the place, with calendar invites for every session, plus the video link if it runs online or the address and arrival notes if it's in a room.

<Note>
  Booking a place also opens messaging with the facilitator, the same as booking a session does. See
  [Messages](/clients/messages).
</Note>

## Waitlists

If a cohort is full you can join its waitlist. **Nothing is charged.** If a place opens in that intake it's offered to you, and you have **48 hours** to take it.

## Withdrawing

**Withdraw before the first session and you're refunded in full.** Email us and we'll refund the lot. After the first session the place is yours, and the program's own terms apply — they're on the program page.

## What's on a program page

About this program, who it's for, what happens in a session, the dates and intakes, the facilitators, and funding and rebates, with other programs like it underneath. The price sits beside it with the next intake, where it runs, and a way through to one-to-one care if that turns out to suit you better.

Some programs are co-facilitated, in which case every facilitator is listed, each linking to their profile.

## Cost

A group place is usually a fraction of the equivalent individual fee, and what you see is what you pay — Open Therapy adds nothing on top.

Facilitators pay a **15% commission** on group enrolments, in place of the fees a one-to-one session carries. That's their cost, not yours. See [Fees](/fees).

## Rebates

Whether a Medicare, NDIS, private health or employer rebate applies depends on the program, and each program page says which. Group programs are not automatically rebatable just because individual sessions with the same practitioner are — see [Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).

## If a group isn't the right shape

A group runs to a schedule and a topic, and neither bends to a hard week. If you're struggling and a program's first session is a fortnight away, don't wait on it — book a session, or see [Getting help](/getting-help) for what's available right now, including **Lifeline** on **13 11 14**.
