Everything booked, attended and cancelled sits under **Sessions** in your workspace, at **/workspace/sessions**.

## The three tabs

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming** | Everything ahead of you, soonest first |
| **Past** | Each with **Book again** and any notes from the room |
| **Cancelled** | Sessions called off, by either side |

Each tab carries a count, and the page names your timezone — **Times in AEST**.

A row shows the service, session mode, duration, day and time, and a status. Inside a repeating booking it says which one — *2 of 6, weekly*.

## Your therapist's terms, on the row

Hover or tap the dotted underline on their name for their cancellation terms, headed **Cancelling with** that therapist. See [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds).

## Moving a session

<Info>
  Rescheduling is live, and it's free. Nothing is charged again.
</Info>

Select **Move**. The dialog lists your therapist's open hours for the next six weeks, grouped by day. Pick one, add a reason if you want, and select **Move the session**.

You can only land on an hour your therapist publishes, and not the one you're in. If nothing is open, the dialog suggests you message them.

<Note>
  Moving needs at least as much notice as cancelling for free. Inside that window, the dialog says so
  and cancelling stays available. See [how the notice is worked
  out](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds#the-notice-you-need-to-move).
</Note>

Your therapist is emailed the new time and the old hour goes back into their diary.

## Cancelling

Select **Cancel** on the row. The cost is worked out before you confirm — see [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds#how-to-cancel).

## Joining an online session

**Room** sits on the row once a video link exists. It becomes **Join** ten minutes before the start and stays until the session ends. Same link every time, and the one in your confirmation email works too.

Inside: microphone, camera, screen share, raise your hand, and two side panels — **Chat** and **Notes**.

<Note>
  Sessions aren't recorded. Recording isn't switched on for Open Therapy rooms at all.
</Note>

If the room won't open, it tells you why: too early, in person rather than online, cancelled, not yet confirmed, or finished. The room closes an hour after the session's end time.

## Your calendar

**Calendar**, at **/workspace/calendar**, is the same information by day, and covers **Sessions**, **Entries** from your journal, **Check-ins**, and **Listens** from the library.

Desktop gets a month grid with the day's agenda beside it, a legend counting each kind, and arrows to page through months. On a phone the months scroll.

## Sessions you don't recognise

Your card statement shows the **therapist's practice name**, not Open Therapy, because they're the merchant of record. Check [Invoices and receipts](/clients/invoices-and-receipts) before disputing a line with your bank.

## Exporting

**Settings** has an export of your whole record, sessions included. See [Your account](/clients/your-account#settings).
