Everything booked, attended and cancelled sits under **Sessions** in your workspace, at **/workspace/sessions**.

## The three tabs

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming** | Everything ahead of you, soonest first |
| **Past** | Sessions that have happened, each with **Book again** and any notes from the room |
| **Cancelled** | Sessions called off, by either side |

Each tab carries a count, and the page says which timezone it's using — **Times in AEST**, or whatever yours is.

A row shows the service, how you're meeting, the duration, the day and time, and a status. Where a session is part of a repeating booking, the row says which one it is — *2 of 6, weekly*.

## Your therapist's terms, on the row

Their name carries a dotted underline. Hover or tap it and you get their cancellation terms as a small card, headed **Cancelling with Sarah** — each window and what it costs. Two therapists means two sets of terms, each on their own row.

See [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds) for what those numbers mean in money.

## Moving a session

<Info>
  Rescheduling is live, and it's free. Nothing is charged again — it's the same session at a different
  hour.
</Info>

Select **Move** on an upcoming session. The dialog opens on **Move this session?**, tells you when it currently is, and lists your therapist's open hours for the next six weeks, grouped by day. Pick one, add a reason if you want to, and select **Move the session**.

You can only land on an hour your therapist publishes, and the hour you're already in isn't offered. If they've nothing else open, the dialog says so and suggests you message them.

<Note>
  Moving needs at least as much notice as cancelling for free does. If you're inside that window, the
  **Move** button still opens, but the dialog tells you what notice was needed and that cancelling is
  still available. See [how the notice is worked
  out](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds#the-notice-you-need-to-move).
</Note>

Your therapist is emailed the new time and the old hour goes back into their diary.

## Cancelling

Select **Cancel** on the row. What it costs is worked out before you confirm — see [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds#how-to-cancel).

## Joining an online session

The **Join** button appears ten minutes before the start and stays up until the session ends. The room is the same link every time, and the one in your confirmation email works too.

Inside, you have your microphone, your camera, screen share, a way to raise your hand, and two side panels — **Chat** and **Notes**.

<Note>
  Sessions aren't recorded. Recording isn't switched on for Open Therapy rooms at all, so there's
  nothing to turn off and no file kept afterwards.
</Note>

If the room won't open, it tells you why: too early, in person rather than online, cancelled, not yet confirmed, or finished. The room closes an hour after the session's end time.

## Your calendar

**Calendar**, at **/workspace/calendar**, is the same information by day rather than by list, and it includes more than sessions: **Sessions**, **Entries** from your journal, **Check-ins**, and **Listens** from the library.

On a desktop you get a month grid with the day's agenda beside it, a legend counting each kind, and arrows to page through months. On a phone the months scroll instead of paging.

## Sessions you don't recognise

Your card statement shows the **therapist's practice name**, not Open Therapy, because they're the merchant of record. If a line looks unfamiliar, check your receipts under [Invoices and receipts](/clients/invoices-and-receipts) before disputing it with your bank.
