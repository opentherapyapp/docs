Everything about your care lives in your workspace, at **/workspace**. Open it from your avatar in the header.

## What's in the sidebar

The page you're on shows as a solid pill.

| Group | Items |
|---|---|
| **Care** | Overview · Sessions · Calendar · Messages · About you · Documents · Forms |
| **Between sessions** | Journal · Prompts · Library |
| **Finding care** | Shortlist · Match preferences |
| **Account** | Billing · Settings |

On a phone the sidebar becomes a floating bar with four slots — **Home**, **Browse**, **Sessions**, **Messages** — with everything else behind your avatar, along with **Sign out** and **Crisis support**. **Home** is the same page as **Overview**.

Press `⌘K` anywhere for the command palette, which searches sessions, library and documents.

These have their own pages: [Messages](/clients/messages), [Documents](/clients/documents), [Forms and measures](/clients/forms-and-measures), [Library](/clients/library), [Prompts](/clients/prompts) and [Journal](/clients/journal). Sessions and Calendar are covered in [Manage your sessions](/clients/manage-your-sessions). The rest are below.

## Overview

Your overview leads with the check-in, a short mood log with your recent ones under it. Then, in order:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming sessions** | The next few, the closest with a countdown, and **All sessions** for the rest. With nothing booked, **Match me** or a link to book with someone you already see |
| Recent journal entries | The last few, with a way into [your journal](/clients/journal) |
| **Your care team** | A card per therapist you're seeing, each with its own way to book |
| **Goals** | Set with your therapist, each with its progress |
| **Your measures** | Your latest score on each questionnaire, and which way it's moved |
| **Your safety plan** | Only if you've written one with your therapist, and only the parts that were filled in |

<Note>
  Your safety plan isn't a crisis service. If you need help right now, call **000**, or **Lifeline** on
  **13 11 14** — see [Getting help](/getting-help#crisis-support).
</Note>

## Shortlist

Therapists you kept while going through your matches, at **/workspace/shortlist**. Nobody on the list has been contacted.

Each card carries the reason that therapist came up, **View profile**, **See times**, and a **Shortlisted** button to remove them. Anyone added straight from the directory has no match behind them, so their card shows what they specialise in.

Shortlisting works signed out at **/shortlist**, and that list lives in the browser until you make an account. Signed in, **/shortlist** takes you to the workspace version. There's no "passed" list.

## Match preferences

Every matching question on one page. Matches are ranked fresh from these answers every time you look, so a change takes effect immediately and your shortlist isn't touched.

To widen a search that's returning nobody, relax the language, the gender or the location answer. See [Find a therapist](/clients/find-a-therapist#changing-your-answers).

## About you

Written once, and every new therapist you see starts from it: your photo and name, a bit about you, who to call in an emergency, your circumstances, your health background, and how to work with you.

What each therapist can see is set under **Privacy** in settings.

## Billing

**Invoices** to claim with, **Payments** for what you've paid, and **Payment methods** for the cards you've saved. Selecting any document opens it with **Print or save as PDF**. See [Invoices and receipts](/clients/invoices-and-receipts).
