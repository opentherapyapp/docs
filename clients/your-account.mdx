Everything about your care lives in your workspace, at **/workspace**. Open it from your avatar in the header.

## What's in the sidebar

The sidebar is grouped rather than one long list, and the page you're on shows as a solid pill.

| Group | Items |
|---|---|
| **Care** | Overview · Sessions · Calendar · Messages · About you · Documents · Forms |
| **Between sessions** | Journal · Prompts · Library |
| **Finding care** | Shortlist · Match preferences |
| **Account** | Billing · Settings |

On a phone the sidebar becomes a floating bar with four slots — **Home**, **Browse**, **Sessions**, **Messages** — and everything else sits behind your avatar, along with **Sign out** and a **Crisis support** link. The bar calls it **Home** where the sidebar says **Overview**; it's the same page.

Press `⌘K` anywhere for the command palette, which searches sessions, library and documents.

Five of these have their own pages here: [Messages](/clients/messages), [Documents](/clients/documents), [Forms and measures](/clients/forms-and-measures), [Library](/clients/library), [Prompts](/clients/prompts) and [Journal](/clients/journal). Sessions and Calendar are covered in [Manage your sessions](/clients/manage-your-sessions). The rest are below.

## Overview

Your overview leads with the check-in — a short mood log you can keep for yourself, with your recent ones under it. It's above your next appointment on purpose: the session matters one day a week, and the check-in is what makes the other six worth opening.

Then, in order:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming sessions** | The next few, the closest with a countdown, and **All sessions** for the rest. With nothing booked you get **Match me** or a link to book with someone you already see |
| Recent journal entries | The last few, with a way into [your journal](/clients/journal) |
| **Your care team** | A card per therapist you're seeing, each with its own way to book. There's no single booking button, because you may have more than one therapist |
| **Goals** | Set with your therapist, each with its progress |
| **Your measures** | Your latest score on each questionnaire, and which way it's moved |
| **Your safety plan** | Only if you've written one with your therapist, and only the parts that were filled in |

<Note>
  Your safety plan is something you write with your therapist. It isn't a crisis service. If you need
  help right now, call **000**, or **Lifeline** on **13 11 14** — see [Getting
  help](/getting-help#crisis-support).
</Note>

## Shortlist

Therapists you kept while going through your matches, at **/workspace/shortlist**. Nobody on the list has been contacted — it's a list for you.

Each card carries the reason that therapist came up, **View profile**, **See times**, and a **Shortlisted** button to take them off again. Anyone you added straight from the directory has no match behind them, so their card shows what they specialise in instead.

Shortlisting works signed out, at **/shortlist**, and that list lives in the browser until you make an account. Once you're signed in, **/shortlist** takes you to the workspace version of the same list. There's no "passed" list any more — scrolling past someone is the whole of turning them down.

## Match preferences

Every matching question on one page. Your matches are ranked fresh from these answers every time you look, so a change takes effect immediately and your shortlist isn't touched.

This is also the fastest way to widen a search that's returning nobody: relax the language, the gender or the location answer. See [Find a therapist](/clients/find-a-therapist#changing-your-answers).

## About you

Written once, and every new therapist you see starts from it: your photo and name, a bit about you, who to call in an emergency, your circumstances, your health background, and how to work with you.

What each therapist can actually see is yours to decide, under **Privacy** in settings.

## Billing

**Invoices** to claim with, **Payments** for what you've paid, and **Payment methods** for the cards you've saved. Selecting any document opens it with **Print or save as PDF**.

See [Invoices and receipts](/clients/invoices-and-receipts) for what's on each one and how they differ.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your details" icon="user">
    Your photo, your name and when you joined, with **Edit** taking you to **About you** — the things you
    write for another person to read live there rather than here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Privacy" icon="lock">
    **What your therapists see**, one row at a time rather than one switch over the lot: your mobile
    number, your age (never your date of birth), your emergency contact and GP, your circumstances, your
    health background, your therapy history, anything else you've written, how to work with you, and your
    outcome measures. Only therapists you've booked or messaged see any of it.

    Under that, **Research** — off unless you turn it on, saved for when research partnerships exist,
    and we'll ask again before any study uses anything.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check ins" icon="heart-pulse">
    A Sunday reminder to record how the week went, a soft sound when you record one, which scales you
    want to track, and your own tags.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Export" icon="download">
    Your record — referrals, letters and worksheets — downloadable any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Library" icon="headphones">
    How the audio library behaves for you. See [Library](/clients/library).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Timezone" icon="globe">
    The zone your session times, reminders and calendar are shown in. Change it when you travel and every
    session time shifts with it. We suggest your browser's zone rather than saving it silently, because a
    wrong zone here moves every appointment you own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notifications" icon="bell">
    **Session reminders** the day before and again shortly before. **New messages**, which emails you
    that a therapist replied and never the message itself. **Forms**, when one is sent and if it falls
    overdue. Each can be turned off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Matching" icon="sparkles">
    A pointer to your match preferences, since that's what your matches are ranked from.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Security" icon="key">
    Set a new password by emailed link, change your email in three steps, and sign out of every device at
    once. See [Create an account](/clients/create-an-account#changing-your-email).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Closing your account" icon="log-out">
    Closing removes your profile. Therapists must keep clinical notes for seven years under Australian
    law — see [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy#your-clinical-record).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Settings save as you change them, and the header tells you when they have.
