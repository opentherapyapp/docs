Sessions booked with the **Online** session mode happen in a video room on Open Therapy. You don't install anything and there's no meeting ID to type in. Sessions booked as **In person** or **Home visit** have no room — the address is in your confirmation email.

## When the room opens

The room opens ten minutes before the start time and closes an hour after the scheduled end. Inside that window the link works; outside it, it doesn't.

| | |
|---|---|
| Earlier than ten minutes before | The page says you're a little early and gives you the time it opens |
| Ten minutes before, until the end | You can join |
| Up to an hour after the scheduled end | The room stays open, so running over doesn't cut you off |
| After that | The room closes and everyone still in it is returned to the app |

The link never changes. It's in your confirmation email and on the booking under **Sessions** in your workspace, and it's the same link every time for that session.

If your therapist hasn't confirmed the booking yet, the room won't open. The page tells you the session isn't confirmed rather than sending you into an empty room.

## The green room

Joining takes you to a check page first. Nobody can see or hear you here.

You get a preview of your own camera, and:

- **Camera** and **Microphone** toggles, so you can start with either off
- A level meter beside your name that moves when you speak — if it stays flat, the microphone the browser picked isn't the one you're talking into
- Pickers for **Camera**, **Microphone** and **Speakers**, listing every device the browser can see
- A connection line that reads **Checking connection**, then **Connection strong**, **Connection patchy** or **Connection poor**

Change devices here rather than mid-session. The toggle states you set carry into the session.

When you're ready, click **Join session**.

## In the session

The timer counts from the scheduled start time, not from when you joined, and runs against the length you booked. Both of you see the same number.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Mute microphone** | Mutes and unmutes you |
| **Turn camera off** | Turns your video off and on |
| **Share your screen** | Shares a window or a tab. The button is hidden on devices whose browser can't share, such as iPhones and iPads |
| **Raise your hand** | Flags that you want to say something, without cutting in |
| **Chat** | A text panel for the two of you, for a link or a spelling |
| **Notes** | Your own private notes |
| **Leave** | Leaves the room |

While you're sharing, a band across the top reminds you that your screen is visible.

Chat exists only while the session is running. It's cleared when the session ends and nothing from it is stored.

Your notes are private to you. They're saved as you type, kept with the session so you can read them later, and hold up to 8,000 characters. Your therapist never sees them, and you never see theirs.

## What is and isn't recorded

Nothing is recorded. There is no recording feature in the room, for either of you — no video, no audio, no transcript. What's kept is the fact that the session happened, how long you were connected, and anything you typed into your own notes.

## If you lose connection

Losing connection doesn't end the session. Close the tab or reload and open the same link again — the room is still there and your therapist is still in it, as long as you're inside the time window.

Two things do end it: your therapist ending the session, which closes the room for both of you, and the room closing an hour after the scheduled end.

If you can't get back at all, message your therapist. Sessions cut short by a technical problem are between the two of you to sort out; see [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds) for how money is handled.

## If the room won't load

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reload the page">
    The room asks the video provider for a spot each time you join. If that request fails you'll see a short message and a **Try again** button. Use it — a second attempt usually works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the browser has permission">
    A flat level meter and a black preview usually mean the browser blocked the camera or microphone rather than the room failing. Check the permission prompt in the address bar, then reload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try another browser">
    An out-of-date browser, or a work laptop that blocks camera access, is a common cause of a room that opens to nothing. Try a current browser you keep updated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to your phone">
    The room works in a phone browser. If your laptop won't cooperate, open the same link there so the session can start on time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell your therapist">
    Message them from **Messages** so they know you're trying rather than absent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the page says video isn't configured, that's on us rather than on your setup. Write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app) with the session date and your therapist's name.
