**Documents** in your workspace is where files about your care live.

| | |
|---|---|
| Accepted types | PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, and Word documents (`.doc`, `.docx`) |
| Maximum size | 25 MB per file |
| Name | Up to 200 characters. Rename it any time |

A photo of a paper referral is fine — flat, in good light, all four corners in frame. Images show as thumbnails; PDFs and Word files show as a page with the file type on it.

## Choosing a type

Every document is filed under a type, which makes it findable later.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Referral** | From a GP or another clinician |
| **Care plan** | A Mental Health Treatment Plan, NDIS plan or similar |
| **Report** | An assessment or discharge summary |
| **Signed form** | Consent, or anything you've signed |
| **Worksheet** | Something to work through |
| **Something else** | Everything that isn't one of the above |

Referrals and care plans can carry an expiry date; the other types have no expiry field. Set it and the document shows **Expires** with the date, then **Expired** once it passes.

Nothing stops working when a referral lapses, and Open Therapy doesn't check your paperwork before you book. Whether a lapse affects a rebate is between you, your GP and Medicare — see [Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).

## Who can see a document

You, and the therapists you're currently seeing on Open Therapy. There's no per-document switch: filing a document here **is** sharing it with your treating therapists. Don't upload what you don't want a therapist to read.

| | |
|---|---|
| You | Everything in your documents, always |
| A therapist you're seeing | Everything in your documents |
| A therapist you only messaged, or haven't booked | Nothing |
| Another client | Nothing |

Files are stored privately, not at a public web address, and a download link is generated when you ask for one.

Uploading doesn't notify anyone, so [message them](/clients/messages) if a therapist needs to read something before your session. You are told when a therapist shares a document with you.

## Documents are not receipts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Documents** | Files you or your therapist upload. Clinical paperwork — referrals, plans, reports, worksheets |
| **Receipts and invoices** | Generated by Open Therapy when a session is paid for. One per settled session, with a reference you can quote |

Don't upload receipts here. They already exist on your billing page — see [Invoices and receipts](/clients/invoices-and-receipts).

## Changing or removing one

Rename a document, change its type, or set and clear an expiry date from the menu on it.

There's no delete button. If you uploaded something by mistake, write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app) with the file name. A document your therapist has already read may form part of the clinical record they're required to keep — [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy) explains what that means.
