**Documents** in your workspace is where files about your care live: a referral from your GP, a mental health treatment plan, an assessment report, a form you've signed, or a worksheet your therapist gave you.

## Uploading a file

Drag a file onto the page, or click **Upload** and pick one.

</Frame>

| | |
|---|---|
| Accepted types | PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, and Word documents (`.doc`, `.docx`) |
| Maximum size | 25 MB per file |
| Name | Up to 200 characters. Rename it any time |

A photo of a paper referral is fine — take it flat, in good light, with all four corners in frame. Images show as thumbnails; PDFs and Word files show as a page with the file type on it.

## Choosing a type

Every document is filed under a type, which is what makes it findable later.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Referral** | From a GP or another clinician |
| **Care plan** | A Mental Health Treatment Plan, NDIS plan or similar |
| **Report** | An assessment or discharge summary |
| **Signed form** | Consent, or anything you've signed |
| **Worksheet** | Something to work through |
| **Something else** | Everything that isn't one of the above |

Referrals and care plans can carry an expiry date, because they're the two that run out. Set it and the document shows **Expires** with the date, then **Expired** once it passes. The other types have no expiry field, since implying they expire would be wrong.

An expiry is for your own benefit — nothing stops working when a referral lapses, and Open Therapy doesn't check your paperwork before letting you book. Whether a lapsed referral affects a rebate is between you, your GP and Medicare; see [Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).

## Who can see a document

You, and the therapists you're currently seeing on Open Therapy.

There's no per-document switch, and that's the thing to understand: filing a document here **is** sharing it with your treating therapists. If you don't want a therapist to read something, don't upload it.

| | |
|---|---|
| You | Everything in your documents, always |
| A therapist you're seeing | Everything in your documents |
| A therapist you only messaged, or haven't booked | Nothing |
| Another client | Nothing |

Files are stored privately, not at a public web address, and a download link is generated for you when you ask for one rather than being a URL somebody could guess or forward.

Uploading doesn't send anyone a notification. If a therapist needs to read something before your session, upload it and then [message them](/clients/messages) to say it's there. When a therapist shares a document with you, you do get told.

## Documents are not receipts

Two different things, in two different places.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Documents** | Files you or your therapist upload. Clinical paperwork — referrals, plans, reports, worksheets |
| **Receipts and invoices** | Generated by Open Therapy when a session is paid for. One per settled session, with a reference you can quote |

Don't upload receipts here. They already exist, with the right numbers and references on them, on your billing page — see [Invoices and receipts](/clients/invoices-and-receipts).

## Changing or removing one

You can rename a document, change its type, or set and clear an expiry date from the menu on it.

There's no delete button. If you uploaded something by mistake or in the wrong place, write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app) with the file name. Note that a document your therapist has already read may form part of the clinical record they're required to keep — [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy) explains what that means.
