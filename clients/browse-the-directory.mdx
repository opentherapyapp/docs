## Starting from what you need instead

**Services** in the header groups the directory by what you're after rather than by who provides it.

<Frame caption="Services groups the directory by what you're looking for rather than by discipline">

Each service page covers what's involved, what it typically costs, whether a rebate applies, and which disciplines deliver it, then hands you to a search for that service near you.

## Searching

Two boxes at the top. Nothing runs until you select **Search** or press Enter.

| Box | What it matches |
|---|---|
| **What do you need help with?** | Name, discipline, focus, specialties, suburb, city, approaches, services |
| **Where** | A suburb or city, or `online` |

Both boxes suggest as you type. Search *ADHD screening* and you'll find a menu that says *ADHD assessment*. Every word must match something, so fewer words find more people.

## Filters

Filters sit in a column beside the results, with **Clear all** at the top. Options inside a group widen your results; options across groups narrow them.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Care type" icon="stethoscope">
    Only care types open for bookings, which today means **Psychology** and **Counselling**. See
    [Care types](/care-types).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Service" icon="list-checks">
    Services rather than disciplines — assessments, initial consultations, couples work — drawn from
    what therapists offer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Availability" icon="clock">
    **This week**, **Weekends**, **Evenings**, **Overnight support**, all read off published hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Session mode" icon="video">
    **Online**, **In person**, and **Comes to you** for home visits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Price" icon="dollar-sign">
    Per session: under \$130, \$130–\$170, \$170–\$220, \$220+. Per hour: under \$80, \$80+. The two
    never mix in the same band.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Funding" icon="badge-percent">
    **Medicare rebate**, **NDIS**, **Private health**, **DVA**, and **Sliding scale**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Gender" icon="users">
    Built from therapists who answered the question. Ticking it hides anyone who hasn't, which is why
    [matching](/clients/find-a-therapist) weights the preference instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Language" icon="languages">
    Built from the languages therapists in the directory speak.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Filters live in the page address, so you can bookmark a search or send it on. A link ending
  `?care=psychology&near=marrickville` opens the same results.
</Tip>

## Sorting

| | |
|---|---|
| **Soonest available** | Default. Taking clients with published hours, then without, then waitlists. Rating breaks ties |
| **Highest rated** | Rating, then how many reviews it rests on |
| **Lowest price** | Session fees ascending, then hourly rates — never interleaved |

The count above the results reads **Showing 12 of 40 therapists** when filters are narrowing things, and just the count when they aren't.

## The map

The map appears once place is in play: a real suburb in **Where**, or an in-person or home-visit filter. Type *online* and it stays a list. The **List** and **Map** toggle forces either.

<Frame caption="In map view the results run down the left and the pins carry the price">
  <img src="/images/browse-map.webp" width="2880" height="1800" style={{ width: 1440 }} alt="The directory in map view, with the List and Map toggle set to Map, therapist cards down the left showing ratings and prices, and a map of the Queensland coast with a single price pin near the Gold Coast" />
</Frame>

| Pin | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pin with a tail** | An address you can attend |
| **Dashed pill with a circle** | The middle of a travel radius. The circle is how far they'll come |

Pins carry the price, merge into a count bubble you can zoom into when they sit close together, and only draw a radius when the pin belongs to one person.

Location matching covers every clinic a therapist works from, plus anywhere they'll travel to.

## On a phone

Filters open as a bottom sheet, and the button at the bottom counts the result — **Show 12 therapists**.

## No results

**Clear all filters** clears the two search boxes as well. Price, gender and language are usually the ones doing the narrowing.
