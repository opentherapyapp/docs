If you'd rather look for yourself than answer questions, **Browse** gives you the full list with everything filterable.

## Searching

Two boxes at the top:

| Box | What it matches |
|---|---|
| **What do you need help with?** | Name, discipline, focus, specialties, suburb, city, and the services a therapist offers |
| **Where** | A suburb or city, or "online" |

Service search understands that practices name the same thing differently. Searching *ADHD screening* will find a therapist whose menu says *ADHD assessment*.

## Filters

Options inside a group widen your results; options across groups narrow them. Choosing both Psychology and Counselling shows you both, but adding a language filter narrows that to therapists who speak it.

<Tip>
  Your filters live in the page address, so you can bookmark a search or send it to someone. A link
  ending `?care=psychology&near=marrickville` opens the same results for them.
</Tip>

## Sorting

- **Soonest available** — the default
- **Highest rated**
- **Lowest price**

## The map

The map appears automatically once you show interest in a place — a suburb in the **Where** box, or an in-person or home-visit filter. You can force it either way with the **List** and **Map** toggle.

| Pin | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Solid** | An address you can attend |
| **Dashed**, with a circle | The centre of a travel radius — the circle is how far they'll come to you |

Pins show the price. Pins close together merge into a count bubble you can zoom into.

Location matching covers *every* clinic a therapist works from, plus anywhere they'll travel to, not just their first listed address.

## On a phone

Filters open as a bottom sheet with a button showing what you'll get — **Show 12 therapists** — so you can see the effect before closing it.

## No results

You'll get a **Clear all filters** button. Price and language are usually the two doing the narrowing.
