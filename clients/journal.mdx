The journal is a private place to write between sessions. It's on every Open Therapy account, whether you've booked a session or not, and it's at **/workspace/journal**.

## Where your entries live

On your account, on the server, so they're there on every device you sign in on.

<Note>
  Entries are private to you. Nothing is visible to a therapist unless you share that particular entry,
  one at a time.
</Note>

If you wrote anything in this browser before there was an account behind it, those entries move onto your account the first time you open the journal here, and you'll be told how many arrived.

## Writing an entry

Select **New entry**. An entry has a date, a title and a body, and it saves as you write.

The editor is a rich text editor rather than a plain box. Select some text and a small menu appears with **Bold**, **Italic**, **Strikethrough**, **Quote** and **Add photo**. Headings work, and on an empty line there's an **Add photo** button in the margin — you can also drag a photo in or paste one.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tags** | Your own, and the same tags your check-ins use, so a word means one thing across both |
| **Folders** | Group entries however you like. The list filters by folder, with **All** first |
| **Share with your care team** | A toggle at the top of the entry. Off means **Private — share with your care team**; on means **Shared with your care team** |

Delete is on the entry itself.

<Note>
  There's no mood field on an entry. The mood log is the **check-in** on your overview — see [Your
  account](/clients/your-account#overview).
</Note>

## Reflections

Open Therapy can reflect on an entry: a few phrases picked out of what you wrote, each with one sentence about it. They appear as **dotted underlines** in your own text, and selecting one opens a small note under that passage.

Two rules make that safe enough to put over a diary:

- **Every quote is verbatim.** A phrase that doesn't appear in your entry exactly as you wrote it is dropped rather than approximated, so nothing is put in your mouth.
- **An entry that reads as acute gets no underlines at all.** A gentle observation in the margin isn't what's wanted, so the reflection stops and points you to a person instead.

Nothing is written into your entry. The underlines sit over your words, and deleting a passage takes its underline with it.

<Warning>
  Reflections are not therapy, not advice and not a diagnosis, and nobody reads your journal on the other
  end of them. If you're in immediate danger, call **000**. For crisis support, call **Lifeline** on
  **13 11 14** — more numbers on [Getting help](/getting-help#crisis-support).
</Warning>

## Prompts

Below your entries sit three prompts, in the order you'd want them: anything your therapist picked out for you first, then ones you've saved, then the rest. Selecting one opens a new entry already titled and seeded with it.

**All prompts** takes you to the full catalogue — see [Prompts](/clients/prompts).

## Sharing with your therapist

Turn the share toggle on and that entry goes to your care team. Everything else stays private, and you can turn it off again.

<Tip>
  Sharing an entry is often more useful than trying to remember a difficult week out loud at the start of
  a session. It also gives your therapist time to think before you're both in the room.
</Tip>

## Streaks and counts

Three cards at the top: **Entries**, **Current streak** and **This month**, the last of which also says how many entries you've shared.

It's there if it helps and ignorable if it doesn't. A broken streak isn't a clinical event, and the card says as much: *one line counts*.
