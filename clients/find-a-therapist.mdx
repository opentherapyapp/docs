Matching asks a short set of questions, then ranks every therapist who fits and puts the reason on each card. Nothing is booked and nobody is told you looked. Start at **Match me** in the header; you don't need an account.

## The questions

Only the first two are required. Signed out, two more screens sit after those — when you'd like to start, and where to send the list — then the rest.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1. Who is the therapy for?" icon="user" defaultOpen>
    One choice: **Individual**, **Couple**, **Teen** (12 to 17), **Child** (under 12), or **Someone I
    care for**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="2. What brings you here?" icon="list">
    As many as fit. This decides which kind of therapist we look at first. Anxiety, low mood, trauma,
    focus and ADHD, burnout, relationships, grief, a big life change, parenting, eating and body image,
    assessment and diagnosis, daily living and NDIS, speech and language, or **Not sure yet**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="3. When would you like to start?" icon="calendar">
    Signed out only, and skippable. **As soon as I can**, **In the next few weeks**, **In a month or
    two**, or **Just looking for now**. Nothing here holds you to a date — it tells us how quickly to
    follow up. Signed in, this screen is skipped.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4. Where should we send these?" icon="mail">
    Signed out only, and optional. Your matches, so they're waiting if you don't finish now. Continue
    works with the field empty. The note under the field reads *"No account needed. We'll only send
    your matches, and you can opt out anytime."* Signed in, this screen is skipped — your address is
    already on file.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="5. Anything running through your story?" icon="heart">
    Eight themes: culture and belonging; family; identity, gender and sexuality; neurodivergence,
    disability and health; faith and community; work, money and study; parenting, fertility and caring;
    loss, harm and survival. **None of these** skips it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="6. Which of these is the work about?" icon="target">
    Only appears if you named a theme, and only shows experiences from the themes you picked. **These
    are weighted hardest.**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="7. What would make this easier?" icon="sliders-horizontal">
    What you'd like in a person (has lived some of this too, gets my culture without a tutorial,
    LGBTQIA+ affirming, neurodiversity-affirming, slow and trauma-informed, direct and practical, warm
    and unstructured), their gender, and your preferred language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="8. The practical bits" icon="map-pin">
    How you'd like to meet (online, in person, home visit), roughly where you are, and how you'll pay
    (Medicare with a GP mental health plan, NDIS, private health extras, or paying yourself). Only
    therapists you can see, afford and get to.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Signed in on your first run, three skippable screens come first: a photo, a bit about you, and your health and context. You're asked once.

## How the ranking works

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| They have **lived** an experience you named | 9 each |
| Your preferred language | 6 |
| Their gender matches what you asked for | 5 |
| They **work with** an experience you named | 4 each |
| Your concerns match their focus or specialties | 3 each |
| A session mode you asked for | 2 |
| A funding route you asked for | 2 |

Ask for someone who *has lived some of this too* or *gets my culture without a tutorial*, and each shared lived experience is worth two more points again.

Ties break on rating, and anyone scoring nothing is dropped. Gender and funding are scored rather than filtered on, so a therapist who hasn't answered still appears — the directory's filters are the strict version.

## Working through your matches

Matches arrive five at a time, most-likely first. **Show more matches** brings the next five.

Each card carries up to two reasons — **Has lived this**, **Works with**, **Speaks** or **Treats** — with the experiences named as chips, falling back to **Specialises in**. The top card is flagged **Closest fit**.

**Shortlist** flips to **Shortlisted**, puts **See times** beside **View profile**, and counts in the corner. Nothing turns someone down; scroll past them.

## Your shortlist

Your shortlist is at **/workspace/shortlist**, or **/shortlist** signed out, and from **Shortlist** in your workspace sidebar. Nobody on it has been contacted.

Shortlisting signed out keeps the list in that browser, and **Keep these** moves it onto a new account. See [Create an account](/clients/create-an-account#if-you-shortlisted-things-before-signing-up).

## Changing your answers

Select **Change what you're looking for** under your matches, or **Match preferences** in your workspace, where every question sits on one page. Matches rank fresh each time, so a change applies immediately, and your shortlist isn't touched.

## If nothing matches

**Widen the search** offers the full directory with your answers carried across as filters, or a way back to loosen a couple of answers.

<Info>
  Five care types can be booked today — **Psychology**, **Counselling**, **Social Workers**, **Mental
  Health Support Workers**, and **Art & Music Therapy**. Some concerns point at care types that aren't
  open yet, such as eating and body image (Dietetics) or speech and language (Speech pathology). Those
  pages offer a waitlist rather than a directory result. See [Care types](/care-types).
</Info>

## What you get by email

Finishing the questions triggers a **Your matches are ready** email carrying the reasons the cards show. Stop part-way and you get one reminder that your answers are still there. Neither email tells any therapist anything.
