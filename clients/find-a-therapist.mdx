Matching asks five questions, then ranks every therapist who fits and puts the reason on each card. Nothing is booked and nobody is told you looked.

Start at **Match me** in the header. You don't need an account.

## The questions

Only the first two are required. Skip the rest and you'll still get a shortlist — you're just handing the ranking less to work with.

    Pick as many as fit. This is what decides which kind of therapist we look at first. The options run

    change, parenting, eating and body image, assessment and diagnosis, daily living and NDIS, speech
    and language — and **Not sure yet**, which is a real answer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="3. Anything running through your story?" icon="heart">
    Eight themes: culture and belonging; family; identity, gender and sexuality; neurodivergence,
    disability and health; faith and community; work, money and study; parenting, fertility and caring;
    loss, harm and survival. Choose **None of these** to skip — that's a finished answer, not a gap.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4. Which of these is the work about?" icon="target">
    Only appears if you named a theme, and only shows experiences from the themes you picked. Not
    everything that's true of you — the handful a therapist would need to understand. **These are
    weighted hardest.**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="5. What would make this easier?" icon="sliders-horizontal">
    Three things: what you'd like in a person (has lived some of this too, gets my culture without a
    tutorial, LGBTQIA+ affirming, neurodiversity-affirming, slow and trauma-informed, direct and
    practical, warm and unstructured), their gender, and your preferred language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="6. The practical bits" icon="map-pin">
    How you'd like to meet (online, in person, home visit), roughly where you are, and how you'll pay
    (Medicare with a GP mental health plan, NDIS, private health extras, or paying yourself). Only
    therapists you can see, afford and get to.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If you're signed in and it's your first run, three short profile screens come first: a photo, a bit about you, and your health and context. All three are skippable, and you're only asked once.

## How the ranking works

Every therapist is scored against your answers, and the weights are deliberate.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| They have **lived** an experience you named | 9 each |
| Your preferred language | 6 |
| Their gender matches what you asked for | 5 |
| They **work with** an experience you named | 4 each |
| Your concerns match their focus or specialties | 3 each |
| A session mode you asked for | 2 |
| A funding route you asked for | 2 |

If you asked for someone who *has lived some of this too* or *gets my culture without a tutorial*, each shared lived experience is worth two more points again.

<Note>
  A single shared lived experience outranks a gender preference, a language match, and any number of
  matching specialties. That's the whole point of the questions — matching on demographics is the
  shallow version, so gender never outranks having been there.
</Note>

Ties break on rating. Anyone who scores nothing at all is dropped. Gender and funding are scored rather than filtered on, so a therapist who hasn't answered those questions still appears — the directory's filters are the strict version if you want it.

## Working through your matches

Your matches arrive as a list, five at a time, most-likely first. **Show more matches** brings the next five.

Each card carries up to two reasons — **Has lived this**, **Works with**, **Speaks** or **Treats** — with the specific experiences named as chips. Where there's nothing personal to say, the card falls back to **Specialises in**. The top card is flagged **Closest fit**.

Select **Shortlist** on anyone worth a second look. The button flips to **Shortlisted**, a **See times** button appears alongside **View profile**, and a counter in the corner links to your shortlist. There's nothing to press to turn someone down — scrolling past them is the whole of it.

## Your shortlist

Your shortlist is at **/workspace/shortlist**, reachable from your matches or from **Shortlist** in your workspace sidebar. Signed out, the same list is at **/shortlist**. Nobody on it has been contacted. It's a list for you.

## Changing your answers

Select **Change what you're looking for** under your matches, or open **Match preferences** in your workspace, where every question sits on one page.

Your matches are ranked fresh from your answers every time you look, so a change takes effect immediately. Your shortlist isn't touched.

## If nothing matches

You'll see **Widen the search**, with two ways on: browse the full directory with your answers carried across as filters, or go back and loosen a couple of answers.

<Info>
  Two care types can be booked today — **Psychology** and **Counselling**. Some concerns point at care
  types that aren't open yet, such as eating and body image (Dietetics) or speech and language (Speech
  pathology). Those therapists appear with a **Coming soon** pill and a waitlist. See
  [Care types](/care-types).
</Info>

## What you get by email

Finishing the questions triggers a **Your matches are ready** email with the same reasons the cards show. Start the questions and stop, and you'll get one reminder that your answers are still there. Neither email tells any therapist anything.
