## What it costs

You pay the therapist's published price and nothing more. There's no booking fee, no surcharge and no membership.

Open Therapy earns from fees charged to the therapist, taken out of their payout. Nothing is added to your bill. See [Fees](/fees) for the full picture.

## Who you can see

Open Therapy lists therapists across fourteen care types. Registration is checked before a profile goes live — see [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy).

<Info>
  Two care types can be booked today: **Psychology** and **Counselling**. The other twelve are marked
  **Soon** and keep their own page, but they don't return therapists in the directory yet — the page
  offers **Tell me when it opens** instead. See [Care types](/care-types).
</Info>

## The three ways to meet

| | |
|---|---|
| **Online** | Secure video, from anywhere. Your link arrives with your confirmation. |
| **In person** | At one of the therapist's locations. The address comes with your confirmation. |
| **Home visit** | Where the therapist offers it, within the area they travel to. |

## Your workspace

Once you have an account, everything lives at **/workspace**: your sessions and calendar, messages, documents, forms, your private journal, your shortlist, and the answers behind your matches. The sidebar groups them under **Care**, **Between sessions**, **Finding care** and **Account**.

## On your phone

There's no app to download. Open Therapy is a website that's built for a phone, and everything you can do on a laptop you can do on one.

<Frame caption="Signed in on a phone, with the navigation bar floating at the bottom of the screen">
  <img src="/images/phone-workspace.webp" width="780" height="1688" style={{ width: 390 }} alt="Open Therapy on a phone, showing a Good morning greeting, a How are you feeling? card, recent check-ins, and a dark floating pill at the bottom holding four icons and the client's avatar" />
</Frame>

Signed in, a small dark bar floats at the bottom of the screen with four places on it — **Home**, **Browse**, **Sessions** and **Messages** — and your face in the last slot. Tapping your face opens the rest: forms, about you, your shortlist, match preferences, library, journal, documents, billing and settings.

Two things make it disappear, both on purpose. It's gone while you're paying, filling in a support request, or in a video session, so nothing floats over the thing you're trying to finish. And on a therapist's profile it slides away to leave the bottom edge to **Book a session**.

<Tip>
  Add Open Therapy to your home screen and it opens without browser chrome, which makes it behave
  like an app. In Safari, tap the share button and choose **Add to Home Screen**; in Chrome, use
  **Install app** in the menu.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Open Therapy is a directory and booking platform, not a crisis service. If you're in immediate
  danger, call **000**. For crisis support, call **Lifeline** on **13 11 14**. More numbers on
  [Getting help](/getting-help#crisis-support).
</Warning>
