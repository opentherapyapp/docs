Open Therapy helps you find a therapist who fits, book with them directly, and keep everything about your care in one place. You can be seen online, at a clinic, or at home.

## What it costs

You pay the therapist's published price and nothing more. No booking fee, no surcharge, no subscription required.

Fees for the therapist come out of their payout, never out of your bill. See [Fees](/fees) if you want the full picture.

Open Therapy lists verified practitioners across fourteen care types, including:

Every one of them has had their registration checked before their profile went live. See [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy).

## The three ways to meet

| Mode | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Online** | Secure video, from anywhere. You get an encrypted link. |
| **In person** | At the therapist's clinic. The address comes with your confirmation. |
| **Home visit** | Where the therapist offers it, within their travel radius. |
