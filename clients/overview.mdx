## What it costs

You pay the therapist's published price plus card processing at checkout. No booking fee, no membership. Open Therapy's introduction fee comes out of the therapist's payout, not yours. See [Fees](/fees).

## Who you can see

Fourteen care types, with registration checked before a profile goes live. See [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy).

<Info>
  Two care types can be booked today: **Psychology** and **Counselling**. The other twelve are marked
  **Soon** and keep their own page, but they don't return therapists in the directory yet — the page
  offers **Tell me when it opens** instead. See [Care types](/care-types).
</Info>

## The three ways to meet

| | |
|---|---|
| **Online** | Secure video. Link arrives with your confirmation |
| **In person** | At one of the therapist's locations. Address arrives with your confirmation |
| **Home visit** | Where offered, within the area they travel to |

## On your phone

No app to download. Open Therapy is a website built for a phone.

<Frame caption="Signed in on a phone, with the navigation bar floating at the bottom">
  <img src="/images/phone-workspace.webp" width="780" height="1688" style={{ width: 390 }} alt="Open Therapy on a phone, showing a Good morning greeting, a How are you feeling? card, recent check-ins, and a dark floating pill at the bottom holding four icons and the client's avatar" />
</Frame>

Signed in, a dark bar floats at the bottom — **Home**, **Browse**, **Sessions**, **Messages** — with your face in the last slot. Tapping it opens forms, about you, shortlist, match preferences, library, journal, documents, billing and settings.

It hides while you're paying, in a support request, or in a video session, and on a therapist's profile it leaves the bottom edge to **Book a session**.

<Tip>
  On your home screen, Open Therapy opens without browser chrome. In Safari, tap share and choose
  **Add to Home Screen**; in Chrome, **Install app**.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Open Therapy is a directory and booking platform, not a crisis service. If you're in immediate
  danger, call **000**. For crisis support, call **Lifeline** on **13 11 14**. More numbers on
  [Getting help](/getting-help#crisis-support).
</Warning>
