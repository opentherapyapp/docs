Cancellation terms belong to the therapist, not to Open Therapy. Each one picks from four policies, and whichever they pick is what appears at checkout, in your confirmation email and on your session row — because it's what the system actually charges.

The terms are copied onto your session when you book, so a therapist changing policy later doesn't change the deal on a session you already have.

## The four policies

| Policy | Cancel free up to | Inside that window | Did not attend |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flexible** | Any time before it starts | No fee | 50% of the fee |
| **Standard** | 24 hours before | 50% of the fee | Full fee |
| **Firm** | 48 hours before | 50% of the fee | Full fee |
| **Strict** | 48 hours before | Full fee | Full fee |

**Standard** is the default and the most common.

<Note>
  Flexible has a twelve-hour window in the table behind it, but its late fee is zero — so the window
  decides nothing and you'll never see it quoted. The product shows two rows for Flexible rather than
  three, because printing "no fee" on both sides of a deadline reads as a mistake rather than as
  generosity. In one line: *"Cancel whenever you need to, no fee. A missed session is charged at 50%."*
</Note>

Some therapists charge nothing for anything, including a missed session. Theirs reads *"Cancel whenever you need to — no fee, and none for a missed session."*

A session booked before policies existed carries no terms at all. Those rows have no **Move** or **Cancel** button, because a button that can't say what it will cost shouldn't be offered — message your therapist instead.

## Two rules that are ours, not theirs

## Moving is free

Rescheduling carries no fee. A move leaves your therapist with the hour sold and you with the session you paid for, so there's nothing to charge for — and nothing to charge it from, since the fee was captured against a session that's still going ahead.

### The notice you need to move

Free moving can't be unlimited, or it becomes the way not to pay a cancellation fee: move to next month, cancel that with a fortnight's notice, and you've done a late cancellation in two steps for nothing. So the notice needed to move is never shorter than the notice needed to cancel for free.

```
notice to move = max(your therapist's reschedule notice, the policy's free window)
```

The default reschedule notice is 24 hours. So on **Standard** you need 24 hours to move, and on **Firm** or **Strict** you need 48 — the policy's own window is the floor. Any move that's allowed therefore leaves more notice than a free cancellation needs, which is why it can't turn a chargeable cancellation into a free one.

Inside that threshold the honest answer is that the hour can't be moved any more. The dialog says *"Moving needs 48 hours notice. You can still cancel, under your therapist's policy."* If the session was already due to start, it says *"This session was due to start, so it can't be moved."*

A therapist may always move their own appointment.

## How to cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the session">
    Find it under **Upcoming** in your sessions list and select **Cancel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the amount">
    The dialog shows exactly what will happen — for example, *"\$105.00 late cancellation fee. The
    remaining \$105.00 goes back on your card."* With no fee, it says *"No fee. \$210.00 goes back on
    your card."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason if you want to">
    Optional, up to 500 characters, and shared with your therapist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The confirm button carries the amount — **Cancel and pay \$105.00** — so you can't cancel without
    seeing the number. With nothing to pay it reads **Cancel the session**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The fee is recalculated on the server when you confirm. If you've had the dialog open a while and
  crossed the window in the meantime, the amount charged is the correct one for the moment you pressed
  the button.
</Note>

Your therapist is told straight away and the slot goes back on their calendar.

## Refunds

Your money goes back to the card you paid with.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cancelled free** | The whole amount is refunded |
| **Cancelled late** | The fee is kept and the remainder refunded |
| **Missed session** | The no-show rate is kept. At the full fee there's nothing to refund |

How long it takes to appear is up to your bank, usually a few business days.

## Missed sessions and rebates

Medicare and private health rebates can't be claimed on a session you didn't attend. A no-show fee is a cancellation charge rather than a health service, so it isn't claimable either.

## Worked example

A \$210.00 session, therapist on **Standard**, with the default 24-hour reschedule notice.

| When you act | Can you move it? | Cancellation fee | Refunded |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 days before | Yes, free | \$0.00 | \$210.00 |
| 25 hours before | Yes, free | \$0.00 | \$210.00 |
| 6 hours before | No — inside the 24-hour notice | \$105.00 | \$105.00 |
| After the start time | No | \$210.00 | \$0.00 |

The same session on **Firm** would need 48 hours to move or to cancel free, and the 25-hour row would cost \$105.00.

## Emails

Cancelling sends a **Cancelled** email to both of you, naming who cancelled, the reason if one was given, the fee that applies and what's on its way back to your card. Moving sends a **Moved** email that leads with the old time as well as the new one, notes that nothing has been charged again, and reminds you the same room link opens ten minutes before the new time.

## If something's gone wrong

If you were charged a fee you don't think applies, write to [billing@opentherapy.app](mailto:billing@opentherapy.app) with the session details. Fees are computed from the terms attached to that specific booking, so we can show you which policy was in force and when the clock started.
