## What you pay

The therapist's published price, and nothing on top. No booking fee, no service charge, no surcharge, no subscription.

<Info>
  There is no platform fee in what you pay. Open Therapy charges the therapist — a one-off introduction
  fee when a new client books them for the first time — and that comes out of their payout, not your
  bill. See [Fees](/fees) for the whole picture.
</Info>

Where a therapist publishes a sliding scale, the reduced fee is offered to you at checkout as a choice between the standard fee and their lower bands. Nothing is checked and there's no paperwork.

## When you're charged

At booking, in full. The money is then held until the session happens.

Holding rather than releasing straight away is what makes a refund possible when a session is cancelled — see [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds).

## What appears on your statement

**The therapist's practice name**, not Open Therapy. Each therapist is the merchant of record for their own sessions.

<Tip>
  Worth knowing before you dispute an unfamiliar line with your bank. Check
  [Invoices and receipts](/clients/invoices-and-receipts) first — every payment has a receipt with the
  therapist's name on it.
</Tip>

## Saved cards

You can ask us to keep a card at checkout, and saved cards appear on your next booking. Nothing is saved unless you ask for it.

**Payment methods** under **Billing** lists what you've saved, with **Remove** on each. Removing a card is permanent, and it doesn't touch a session that's already booked and paid for.

## Gift cards

There's a **Gift card code** field at checkout. Codes look like `OT-XXXX-XXXX`, and the balance comes off the total — the summary shows the reduced amount and the price it came down from. If the card covers the whole fee, there's nothing to pay and you go straight to your confirmation.

## GST

There isn't any on your session. Health services of this kind are GST-free in Australia, so a session invoice carries no GST line at all.

## If a payment fails

You'll get a **Payment failed** email with the amount, what it was for, the reason your bank gave, and what to do next. A failed payment means the session isn't confirmed, so fix the card and book again.

## Card security

Open Therapy never stores your card number. Payments run through Stripe.

## Paying with Medicare, NDIS or private health

You pay in full at booking and claim your rebate yourself. An invoice is issued after each session with what a claim needs, including the MBS item number where the service has one.

Bulk billing and direct claiming aren't available yet. See [Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).

## Group programs

A group place is charged in full when you enrol, and refunded in full if you withdraw before the first session. Some programs are priced for the whole program and some per session — the card says which. See [Groups and programs](/clients/groups).

## Questions about a charge

Write to [billing@opentherapy.app](mailto:billing@opentherapy.app) and include the reference from your receipt.
