## What you pay

The therapist's published price, plus card processing at checkout. No booking fee, no service charge, no subscription.

On a \$180 session the processing line is **\$3.42**, so the card is charged **\$183.42**. The checkout summary writes it as the session plus card processing.

<Info>
  No platform fee is in what you pay. Open Therapy charges the therapist a one-off introduction fee on
  a new client's first booking, out of their payout. Card processing is the Stripe estimate added so
  the therapist keeps the published price. See [Fees](/fees).
</Info>

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sliding scale** | Checkout offers the standard fee or the therapist's lower bands. Nothing is checked |
| **GST** | None. Health services of this kind are GST-free in Australia, so a session invoice carries no GST line |
| **Your statement** | The therapist's practice name, not Open Therapy — each therapist is the merchant of record. Every payment has a [receipt](/clients/invoices-and-receipts) with their name on it |
| **Card security** | Open Therapy never stores your card number. Payments run through Stripe |

## When you're charged

At booking, in full. The money is held until the session happens. See [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds).

## Saved cards

Ask us to keep a card at checkout and it appears on your next booking. Nothing is saved unless you ask.

**Payment methods** under **Billing** lists them, with **Remove** on each. Removing is permanent and doesn't touch a session already booked and paid for.

## First-appointment offer

You may be offered **\$40 off** a first appointment, funded by Open Therapy rather than by the therapist. Enter the code in **Offer code (optional)** at checkout. Codes look like `OT40-XXXX-XXXX`, last 60 days, and apply once. You can't use an offer code and a gift card on the same booking.

## Gift cards

<Info>
  Gift cards are being rolled out. The field at checkout may not appear yet.
</Info>

Enter your code in **Gift card code** at checkout. Codes look like `OT-XXXX-XXXX`, and the balance comes off the total — the summary shows the reduced amount and the price it came down from. If it covers the whole fee, there's nothing to pay.

## If a payment fails

A **Payment failed** email gives the amount, what it was for, the reason your bank gave, and what to do next. The session isn't confirmed, so fix the card and book again.

## Paying with Medicare, NDIS or private health

Pay in full at booking and claim your rebate yourself. Your invoice carries what a claim needs, including the MBS item number where the service has one. Bulk billing and direct claiming aren't available yet — see [Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).

## Group programs

Charged in full when you enrol, and refunded in full if you withdraw before the first session. Some programs are priced for the whole program and some per session — the card says which. See [Groups and programs](/clients/groups).

## Questions about a charge

Write to [billing@opentherapy.app](mailto:billing@opentherapy.app) with the reference from your receipt.
