Booking runs from the panel on a therapist's profile through a two-step checkout. You need an account, and you can make one inside it.

## Choose your slot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a service">
    Each service has its own duration and price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how you'll meet">
    Online, in person, or a home visit, from whichever the therapist offers. Changing this clears your
    time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick where">
    For in-person work, which clinic. For a home visit, your suburb — or **Somewhere else**, in which
    case message them before booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date and time">
    On the hour, grouped into **Morning**, **Afternoon** and **Evening**, in your own timezone with the
    zone named underneath.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Where a Medicare rebate applies, the panel also shows **Your gap with a Medicare care plan** under the price.

## Checkout

### Step 1 — your details

Optional: your phone number, and **What would you like to work on?**, which goes to the therapist before the session. A **Gift card code** field sits here too.

A published sliding scale offers the standard fee or reduced bands, each with the household income it's for. Nothing is checked and no paperwork is asked for.

<Info>
  No account yet? Sign up in this page — name, email, six-digit code — with the card form underneath.
  See [Create an account](/clients/create-an-account#signing-up-during-a-booking).
</Info>

### Step 2 — payment

Your card is charged in full now and held until the session happens. Saved cards appear here.

The summary lists the therapist, service, duration, when, how, where, the total, a **Change service or time** link, and the cancellation terms. The total is the session plus card processing — written as *\$180.00 session + \$3.42 card processing*. A gift card or reduced fee names the original price.

<Note>
  Paying holds the hour for **15 minutes**. The page counts down: *"This time is held for you
  for 14:59. After that it goes back to whoever wants it."* If the clock runs out, the hour is
  released and you'll need another slot.
</Note>

A gift card covering the whole fee takes you straight to the confirmation.

## Booking rules

| | |
|---|---|
| Minimum notice | **24 hours** before the session starts, by default |
| Furthest ahead you can book | **2 months**, by default |
| Slot length | On the hour |
| Price and duration | Set by the therapist and applied server-side |

Both defaults are the therapist's to change, along with any gap either side. The calendar pages past the booking window, but those days carry no times.

Two people can't buy the same hour. Lose a slot at checkout and you're told — *"That time was just taken. Choose another slot."* Reloading the payment page returns you to the same payment rather than charging twice.

## Confirmation

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Your session is booked">
    The therapist, service, when, how, where and what you paid, with links to your sessions and to what
    happens next. Your confirmation email carries the video link or the address, and the cancellation
    terms in writing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Confirming your payment">
    Some payment methods take a few seconds to settle. The page refreshes itself, and you can close it
    — the confirmation is emailed either way and nothing is charged twice.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

What arrives depends on how you're meeting:

| Mode | What arrives |
|---|---|
| Online | An encrypted video link, opening ten minutes before the start. Same link every time |
| In person | The clinic address and arrival notes |
| Home visit | Your street address confirmed before the visit |

## When you can't book

| | |
|---|---|
| **Not taking new clients** | Their profile offers a waitlist, or says plainly that they're closed |
| **Payments aren't set up on their side** | You'll be told, and invited to message them. Nothing is wrong with your account |
| **The care type isn't open yet** | Five care types can be booked today. Others offer a waitlist — see [Care types](/care-types) |
| **They arrange work by the hour** | Support work is requested rather than booked, below |

## Booking with a support worker

Support workers are engaged by the hour on a roster. Their page has a request form instead of a checkout — see [Therapist profiles](/clients/therapist-profiles#support-workers-are-different).
