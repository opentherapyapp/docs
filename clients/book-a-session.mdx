Booking runs from the panel on a therapist's profile through a two-step checkout. You need an account, and you can make one inside the checkout without leaving it.

## Choose your slot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a service">
    Each service has its own duration and price. An initial consultation is often longer and priced
    differently from a review session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how you'll meet">
    Online, in person, or a home visit, from whichever the therapist offers. Changing this clears your
    time, because it moves the appointment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick where">
    For in-person work, which clinic. For a home visit, your suburb — or **Somewhere else** if you're
    outside their usual round, in which case message them before booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date and time">
    Times sit on the hour and are grouped into **Morning**, **Afternoon** and **Evening**, shown in your
    own timezone with the zone named underneath.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Frame>

If the therapist offers a Medicare rebate on that service, the panel also shows **Your gap with a Medicare care plan** under the price.

## Checkout

### Step 1 — your details

Your phone number, which is optional, and **What would you like to work on?**, also optional — that box goes to the therapist before the session. There's a **Gift card code** field here too.

If your therapist publishes a sliding scale, you'll see it offered as a choice between the standard fee and their reduced bands, each with the household income it's for. Nothing is checked and you won't be asked for paperwork.

<Info>
  No account yet? The signup happens in this page: your name, your email, and a six-digit code, with the
  card form appearing underneath. See [Create an
  account](/clients/create-an-account#signing-up-during-a-booking).
</Info>

### Step 2 — payment

Your card is charged in full now, and the money is held until the session happens. If you've paid on Open Therapy before, your saved cards appear here.

The summary beside the form shows the therapist, service, duration, when, how, where and the total, a **Change service or time** link, and the cancellation terms in one line. Where a gift card or a reduced fee has brought the total down, it says so and names the original price.

<Warning>
  Your slot isn't held until you pay. If you leave checkout to think about it, someone else can take the
  time.
</Warning>

If a gift card covers the whole fee, there's nothing to pay and you go straight to the confirmation.

## Booking rules

| | |
|---|---|
| Minimum notice | **24 hours** before the session starts, by default |
| Furthest ahead you can book | **2 months**, by default |
| Slot length | On the hour |
| Price and duration | Set by the therapist and applied server-side |

Both defaults are the therapist's to change, along with any gap they want either side of a session. The calendar in the booking panel will page further ahead than the booking window, but there'll be no times on those days.

Two people can't buy the same hour. If a slot goes while you're at checkout you'll be told — *"That time was just taken. Choose another slot."* — rather than double-booked. Reloading the payment page returns you to the same payment rather than charging you twice.

## Confirmation

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Your session is booked">
    You'll see the therapist, service, when, how, where and what you paid, with links to your sessions
    and to what happens next.

    Your confirmation email carries the video link or the address, and the therapist's cancellation
    terms in writing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Confirming your payment">
    Some payment methods take a few seconds to settle, so you may briefly see the booking still
    confirming. You can close the page — the confirmation is emailed either way and nothing is charged
    twice. The page refreshes itself while it waits.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

What arrives depends on how you're meeting:

| Mode | What arrives |
|---|---|
| Online | An encrypted video link. The room opens ten minutes before the start, and it's the same link every time |
| In person | The clinic address and arrival notes |
| Home visit | Your street address confirmed before the visit |

## When you can't book

| | |
|---|---|
| **The therapist isn't taking new clients** | Their profile offers a waitlist instead, or says plainly that they're closed |
| **Payments aren't set up on their side** | You'll be told, and invited to message them. Nothing is wrong with your account |
| **The care type isn't open yet** | Only Psychology and Counselling can be booked today. Other care types offer a waitlist — see [Care types](/care-types) |
| **They arrange work by the hour** | Support work is requested rather than booked, below |

## Booking with a support worker

Disability support workers are engaged by the hour on a roster rather than booked by the appointment. Their page has a request form instead of a checkout — see [Therapist profiles](/clients/therapist-profiles#support-workers-are-different).
