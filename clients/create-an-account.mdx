You don't need an account to search, read profiles or shortlist. You do need one to book, message or write anything down.

## Sign up

<Frame caption="The first step of signup, choosing who the account is for">
  <img src="/images/signup.webp" width="2880" height="1800" style={{ width: 1440 }} alt="The Open Therapy signup page headed Who's this for?, offering I'm looking for therapy or I'm a therapist, with a Log in link underneath and a panel of photographs alongside" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose who the account is for">
    Under **Who's this for?**, pick **I'm looking for therapy**. You can add the therapist role later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your name and email">
    First name, last name and email. **Google** and **Apple** sign-in, where available, skip the next
    step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    We email a six-digit code, which **expires in 30 minutes** and works once. Enter it and select
    **Confirm email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a password">
    At least **8 characters**, then **Create account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  No code after a minute? Select **Send a new code** and check spam. The subject line is the code
  itself — *"123456 is your Open Therapy code"*.
</Tip>

You land on the matching questionnaire, or back in your booking if you were part-way through one.

## Signing up during a booking

You can make an account inside checkout: name, email and the six-digit code, with the card form underneath — see [Book a session](/clients/book-a-session). It skips the password; set one from your confirmation screen or **Settings**.

<Note>
  Once you reach payment, the hour is held for 15 minutes while you finish. See
  [Book a session](/clients/book-a-session).
</Note>

## Sign in

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email and password">
    Tick **Keep me signed in** to stay signed in.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Email a code">
    Select **Use a code instead** for a six-digit code, good for 30 minutes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Select **Forgot password** for a reset link, also valid for 30 minutes.

## If you shortlisted things before signing up

Anything you did signed out is kept in your browser and moves onto your account when you sign in:

- Therapists you shortlisted
- Your answers to the matching questions
- Journal entries written in that browser

## Account emails

| | |
|---|---|
| `123456 is your Open Therapy code` | Signing up, asking for a code, or changing your email |
| `Set a new password` | You request a password reset |
| `Your password was changed` | Your password changes |
| `Your email was changed` | Your email address changes |
| Security notice | A sign-in method is added or changed |

Each ends with a line telling you what to do if it wasn't you.

## Changing your email

From **Settings**, under **Security**. Three steps, in order: a code to your current address, then the new address, then a code to the new inbox. Nothing changes until the last code is confirmed.

## Signing out everywhere

Also under **Security**. It ends every session on every device, including the one you're using.

## Closing your account

**Settings** has an export of your record and, at the bottom, **Close my account**, which opens a request to our support team.

Closing removes your profile. Therapists must retain clinical notes for seven years under Australian law — see [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy#your-clinical-record).
