<Info>
  Every session booked through Open Therapy is paid in full at booking, and you claim your rebate
  yourself. An invoice is issued after each session with what a claim needs. Direct claiming is being
  built.
</Info>

## What this means in practice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pay in full at booking">
    Your card is charged the therapist's published price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect your invoice">
    It appears under **Billing** in your workspace after the session, with the MBS item number where the
    service has one. See [Invoices and receipts](/clients/invoices-and-receipts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim it yourself">
    Lodge it with Medicare, your private health fund, or your NDIS plan manager.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Seeing the gap before you book

Where a therapist has attached an MBS item to a service, the booking panel shows **Your gap with a Medicare care plan** under the price, and your billing list shows the amount you should get back on each invoice.

Those figures come from the Medicare Benefits Schedule rather than from a number a therapist typed, so they move when the schedule is indexed. They're what the schedule pays for that item — your own claim depends on your plan.

## Medicare

To claim a Medicare rebate for psychological services you generally need a **Mental Health Treatment Plan** from your GP before your first session, and the therapist must be eligible to provide services under it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="See your GP">
    Ask about a Mental Health Treatment Plan. The appointment usually involves a short assessment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the therapist accepts Medicare">
    Their profile lists funding types with a tick or a dash under **Rebates and funding**. You can also
    filter the directory by **Medicare rebate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give them your plan and referral">
    Upload it under **Documents** in your workspace, or bring it to your first session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim after each session">
    Lodge the invoice through the Medicare app, myGov, or at a Medicare office.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A Mental Health Treatment Plan covers a set number of subsidised sessions per calendar year, and your GP
  reviews it part-way through. Rebates can't be claimed on a session you didn't attend.
</Note>

## Private health extras

If your policy includes extras cover for the discipline you're seeing, you can usually claim part of the fee. Annual limits apply, and you can't claim both Medicare and private health for the same session.

Check the therapist's profile for whether they're recognised by your fund, and your policy for what's left of your limit.

## NDIS

Whether you can use NDIS funding depends on how your plan is managed:

| Plan management | Booking through Open Therapy |
|---|---|
| **Self-managed** | Pay in full and claim through the myplace portal |
| **Plan-managed** | Pay in full and send the invoice to your plan manager |
| **Agency-managed** | Not supported at checkout. Arrange it with the practitioner |

Disability support workers are engaged differently — by roster and service agreement, invoiced after each shift, with plan managers billed direct. See [Therapist profiles](/clients/therapist-profiles#support-workers-are-different).

## DVA

Some therapists accept DVA White and Gold Cards, and you can filter the directory by **DVA** to find them. Because checkout charges the published fee, arrange a DVA-funded session with the practitioner directly rather than booking it here — message them from their profile.

## Not available yet

To be plain about it, so you don't plan around something that isn't there:

- Bulk billing
- Direct Medicare claiming at the point of payment
- Direct NDIS agency invoicing
- WorkCover bookings

If you need one of these, arrange the session with the practitioner outside Open Therapy, or write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app).

## Finding therapists by funding

Filter the directory by **Funding** — Medicare rebate, NDIS, Private health, DVA, or **Sliding scale** for therapists who publish reduced fees. See [Browse the directory](/clients/browse-the-directory#filters).

<Note>
  Open Therapy can't tell you what your plan covers or what you'll get back. For anything about your own
  tax position or your plan, talk to your accountant or your plan manager.
</Note>
