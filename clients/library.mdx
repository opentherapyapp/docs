The library is guided audio you can listen to between sessions — short recordings for sleep, breathing, grounding and the rest. It's in **Library** in your workspace.

## It's free

Every session in the library is free to anyone with an Open Therapy account. Nothing in it is held back, there's no paid tier, and there's no membership to join — Open Therapy+ was retired and what it used to unlock is simply open. You need an account because the audio isn't published at a public address, not because anyone has paid.

## What's in it

A new session is published every day, so the catalogue grows by one each morning. **Today's pick** sits at the top of the page and is the same for everybody that day.

Sessions are filed under ten topics: Sleep & wind-down, Body scan, Breath & grounding, Anxiety relief, Self-compassion, Focus & clarity, Morning intention, Gratitude, Stress reset and Letting go. Use the pills across the top to filter, or leave it on **For you**.

## Series

A series is a set of sessions meant to be taken in order, released one or two a week rather than all at once. Open one and you get the list, a **Your progress** count, and a single button that reads **Start session 1** the first time and **Continue** after that.

You aren't held to the order. Any session in a series can be played on its own.

## Picking up where you left off

Your position is saved as you listen — every ten seconds while it's playing, and again whenever you pause or stop. Anything you started but didn't finish appears under **Continue listening**, most recent first, and opening it resumes rather than restarts.

A session counts as finished once you've heard almost all of it, so stopping a few seconds before the end still marks it done. Play a finished session again and it starts from the beginning.

Because positions are saved to your account rather than to the browser, you can start something on a laptop and finish it on a phone. What you listened to also shows on your own **Calendar**, alongside your sessions, journal entries and check-ins.

## Background sound

You can put a looping background sound under a session: ocean waves, gentle rain, forest, mountain stream, night crickets or a crackling fire. There's a control in the expanded player and a **Background sound** panel in your settings with a level slider.

The level is per device, so the setting that suits headphones at night isn't forced onto a laptop speaker at lunch.

## Listening offline

You can't. Sessions stream and there's no download, so you need a connection. If you'll be without one, that's worth knowing before a flight.

## What your therapist can see

Nothing. Your listening isn't part of anything a therapist sees — not which sessions you played, not how far you got, not how often you open the library. There's no report and no shared progress.

If you want them to know you've been using it, tell them. See [Messages](/clients/messages).
