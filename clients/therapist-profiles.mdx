A profile carries everything a therapist wants you to know before you book, in a fixed order.

## The header

Their name, a **Shortlist** button, the star rating and how many reviews it rests on, pronouns if given, discipline, and where they work — a suburb and city, or *"3 locations in Sydney"*. Below that, their portrait, video clips and photos of the rooms.

## What's on the page

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="About" icon="file-text" defaultOpen>
    Their bio in their own words.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Services" icon="list">
    Each named service with its duration and price. Selecting one loads it into the booking panel. A
    **Meet and greet** — a short, free online call — sits at the top when they offer one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How I work" icon="compass">
    The modalities they practise in — EMDR, ACT, schema therapy and the rest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Blog" icon="newspaper">
    Posts they've written about the work, with a link to the rest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Groups they run" icon="users">
    Courses, workshops and ongoing groups. You book a place rather than an hour — see
    [Groups and programs](/clients/groups).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Location or locations" icon="map-pin">
    Clinics with their standing days, or a service area on a map with the home-visit radius.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Focus areas" icon="target">
    The specialties they list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Has lived" icon="heart">
    Experiences this therapist has been through themselves, not only trained in. Their own disclosure,
    and what [matching](/clients/find-a-therapist#how-the-ranking-works) weights hardest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Also works with" icon="sparkles">
    Training and caseload beyond their headline focus.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Qualifications" icon="graduation-cap">
    Registering body, registration status, years practising, and qualifications with dates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rebates and funding" icon="badge-percent">
    Each funding type with a tick or a dash and a line of detail, plus any sliding scale. See
    [Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reviews" icon="star">
    Reviews cover the service — booking, admin, premises, manner — and by law can't discuss treatment
    or outcomes. See [Reviews](/clients/reviews).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  **Has lived** and **Also works with** are never merged. Having been through something is not the
  same as having treated it.
</Note>

## The booking panel

On the right, following you down the page as a bar once it scrolls out of view. Choose a service, a mode, a location, then a date and time. See [Book a session](/clients/book-a-session).

<Frame caption="Every service is named with its duration and price">
  <img src="/images/profile-booking-panel.webp" width="758" height="2158" style={{ width: 379 }} alt="The booking panel with Meet and greet selected at 15 minutes and Free, then paid services showing the Medicare gap as the figure and the listed price underneath as at booking, Online selected beside In person, an August calendar with no bookable days while online times are unpublished, the line that there are in person times if you switch, and buttons reading Choose a service and Message Nicholas" />
</Frame>

Two trust lines sit under it: **Registration checked by Open Therapy**, and for online work, **Encrypted video link for online sessions**. **Message Nicholas** asks a question without booking.

Cancellation terms aren't on the profile. They're at checkout, in your confirmation email, and on your session row afterwards.

On a phone it becomes a bar pinned to the bottom with the therapist's name, their lowest price, and **Book** — the one place the navigation bar gets out of the way.

<Frame caption="On a phone the booking panel becomes a bar across the bottom of the screen">
  <img src="/images/phone-profile.webp" width="780" height="1688" style={{ width: 390 }} alt="A therapist profile on a phone, showing the name, rating, pronouns and discipline above a portrait, with a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen reading Book with Nicholas, From \$120, and a Book button" />

The panel changes shape with where the therapist is up to:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Taking clients** | The full booking panel, with a small waitlist form under it |
| **Full** | A waitlist form instead of the panel |
| **Closed to new clients** | A panel saying so, with no form |
| **Care type not open yet** | A waitlist, with **Tell me when it opens** |

## Support workers are different

Disability support workers and other shift-based practitioners are engaged by the hour on a roster rather than booked by the appointment. Their panel shows:

- A weekday hourly rate
- Each support type with its minimum hours
- Rate bands for evenings, weekends and public holidays
- Their usual roster, and the notice they need for a new one
- Where they travel

The button is **Request support**, not a checkout. No payment is taken, and they confirm the roster first.

<Note>
  These practitioners bill after each shift rather than at booking, and plan managers are billed direct.
</Note>

## Personal booking links

A therapist may hand out a link like `opentherapy.link/sarah-chen`. It opens the same profile, minus the similar therapists at the bottom.

## Similar therapists

Three alternatives at the bottom of the page, for when someone is right in kind but wrong on price, location or availability.
