A profile is everything a therapist wants you to know before you book, in a fixed order so you can compare two of them without hunting.

## The header

Name, star rating with the number of reviews behind it, discipline, and location — either a suburb and city, or *"3 locations in Sydney"* if they work from several.

A **verified badge** means Open Therapy has checked their registration against the relevant national register. See [Safety and privacy](/safety-and-privacy#verifying-therapists).

## What's on the page

  <Accordion title="Media" icon="image" defaultOpen>
    A portrait, and often short video clips and photos of the rooms. Worth two minutes — hearing
    someone talk tells you more than a bio.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="About" icon="file-text">
    Their bio in their own words, how they'll meet you, and the languages they speak.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Services" icon="list">
    Each named service with its duration and price. Selecting one loads it into the booking panel, so
    the price you see is the price you'll pay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Updates" icon="newspaper">
    Posts the therapist has written about the work, and practice news when something changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Groups they're running" icon="users">
    Group programs they facilitate. Usually a fraction of the individual fee — see
    [Groups and programs](/clients/groups).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where you'll be seen" icon="map-pin">
    Clinics with their standing days, or a service area on a map with the radius they'll travel for
    home visits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Focus areas" icon="target">
    The specialties they list. Useful for checking your reason for coming is actually their work
    rather than something they'd accept.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Qualifications" icon="graduation-cap">
    Registering body, registration status, years practising, and their listed qualifications with
    dates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rebates and funding" icon="badge-percent">
    Each funding type with a tick or a dash and a line of detail. See
    [Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reviews" icon="star">
    Rating, quoted review, name and date. Reviews cover the service — booking, admin, premises,
    manner — and by law can't discuss treatment or outcomes. See [Reviews](/clients/reviews).
  </Accordion>

## The booking panel

On the right, and it follows you down the page as a bar once it scrolls out of view. You'll choose a service, then a mode, then a location, then a date and time. See [Book a session](/clients/book-a-session).

Two trust lines sit under it: **Registration checked by Open Therapy**, and for online work, **Encrypted video link for online sessions**.

The therapist's cancellation terms are shown here in one line — the same terms that will be enforced if you need to cancel.

## Support workers are different

Disability support workers and other shift-based practitioners are engaged by the hour on a roster, not booked by the appointment. Their profile shows an hourly rate and a **request** form instead of a booking panel.

Nothing is booked or charged from that form. They'll confirm whether the roster works, usually within two business days, and you'll both sign a service agreement before the first shift.

<Note>
  These practitioners bill after each shift rather than at booking, and plan managers are billed
  direct.
</Note>

## Personal booking links

A therapist may hand out a link like `opentherapy.link/sarah-chen` on a card or in an email signature. It opens exactly the same profile. Both addresses are legitimate.

## Similar therapists

Three alternatives at the bottom of every profile. Useful when someone is right in kind but wrong on price, location or availability.
