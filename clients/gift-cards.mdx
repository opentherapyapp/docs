A gift card is an amount of credit with a code attached. Whoever holds the code can put it towards the cost of a session with any therapist on Open Therapy. It doesn't book anything and it doesn't pick anyone — the recipient chooses their own therapist.

## Buying one

You need an Open Therapy account to buy a gift card, because the card is issued against it.

  <img src="/images/gift-cards.webp" width="2880" height="1800" alt="The Open Therapy gift cards page headed Give someone a session, not a scented candle, with a preview of the card marked redeemable with any therapist and no expiry, a grid of preset amounts with a custom option, and a Continue to payment button." />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an amount">
    Pick one of the suggested amounts or type your own. The minimum is \$50 and the maximum is \$1,000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the recipient">
    Their name and email address. You can also write a message of up to 2,000 characters, which goes in the email with your name on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it arrives">
    Send it now, or pick a date. Scheduled cards are sent on the day you chose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    You pay by debit or credit card. The purchase is with Open Therapy rather than with a therapist, so nothing about it is attached to a practice or a person.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card only becomes usable once the payment clears. If the payment fails, no code is issued and nothing is sent. Once it clears you get your own confirmation email, and the recipient gets theirs with your message and your name on it.

## The code

Codes look like `OT-A7KD-3M9P-QX42`: the prefix `OT-`, then twelve characters in groups of four. The alphabet leaves out `0`, `1`, `I` and `O`, so there's nothing to squint at when someone reads a code off a screen.

Each code is unique and is generated when the card is issued. Keep the email — the code is the card.

## Using one at booking

Enter the code in **Gift card code (optional)** on the booking page, before you pay. The credit comes off the session price and your payment card covers the rest.

| | |
|---|---|
| The gift card covers the whole session | Nothing is charged to your payment card, and you're not asked for one |
| It covers part of it | The gift card balance goes first, and only the remainder is charged to your payment card |
| It's worth more than the session | Only what the session costs is taken. The rest stays on the gift card for next time |
| The code is wrong or already spent | You're told before you pay, and the booking waits |

One code per booking. If you're holding two cards, use one now and the other on your next session.

If the balance runs out between the price being quoted and the payment going through — because somebody spent the same code elsewhere in the meantime — the booking fails rather than going ahead half-paid. You're told the card has been drained.

## What the receipt says

You get a receipt either way, and **Paid with** tells you which.

| | |
|---|---|
| The gift card covered the whole session | **Paid with: Gift card**, for the full session price |
| It covered part of it | The amount charged to your payment card, with that card named |

See [Invoices and receipts](/clients/invoices-and-receipts) for where receipts live and what they're good for.

## Expiry

Gift cards don't expire. There's no date on the card and no fee for leaving a balance sitting there.

## If a session is cancelled

<Info>
  Returning gift card credit after a cancellation isn't automated yet. If a session a gift card paid
  for is cancelled or refunded, write to [billing@opentherapy.app](mailto:billing@opentherapy.app)
  with the code and the session date and we'll put the credit back on the card by hand. Anything
  charged to your payment card is refunded the usual way, described in
  [Cancellations and refunds](/clients/cancellations-and-refunds).
</Info>

## If the recipient never got the email

Check the address you typed, then look in their spam folder — the email arrives from Open Therapy, not from you, so it can land there. If it's genuinely missing, write to [support@opentherapy.app](mailto:support@opentherapy.app) with the recipient's email address and the date you bought it, and we'll resend it.
