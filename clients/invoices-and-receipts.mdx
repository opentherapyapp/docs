You get two documents for a session. A **receipt** proves you paid. An **invoice** is what you claim with.

## The difference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Receipt** | Written the moment your card clears, which is at booking. References look like `OTR-2026-0093` |
| **Invoice** | Raised after the session has happened. References look like `OT-2026-0142` |

<Note>
  A late cancellation produces no invoice. The refund is recorded on the receipt instead.
</Note>

## Where they are

Open **Billing** in your workspace, at **/workspace/billing**.

**Invoices** come first, each line showing the service, the date, the therapist, the reference, the amount, and what you should get back where a rebate is recorded. **Payments** below lists your receipts, net of anything refunded. Selecting either opens the full document, with **Print or save as PDF**.

## What's on them

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Reference | Quote it if you contact us |
| Issued | The document's own date, always in Sydney time |
| Status | See below |
| From | Your therapist, as the supplier |
| Billed to | You |
| Description | The service, as the therapist named it |
| Amount | What you paid, net of any refund |
| Paid with | On a receipt, the card it went through |
| MBS item number | On an invoice, where the service has one. The field Medicare reads |
| GST | Nothing. Health services of this kind are GST-free |

<Note>
  No rebate amount is printed on the invoice. What Medicare gives back depends on your plan and your
  claim, neither of which we hold.
</Note>

## Statuses

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Invoices">
    **Due**, **Processing**, **Paid**, **Void** or **Refunded**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Receipts">
    **Paid**, **Partly refunded** or **Refunded**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A partly refunded session — a late cancellation, for example — shows both numbers: what was paid, and what was returned.

## Using an invoice for a claim

The invoice carries what Medicare, a private health fund or an NDIS plan manager needs: the practitioner, the service, the date and the amount you paid. See [Rebates and funding](/clients/rebates-and-funding).

<Tip>
  Save the PDF at the time rather than at tax time.
</Tip>

## Why the therapist's name is on it

Each therapist is the merchant of record for their own sessions, which is why their practice name — not Open Therapy — appears on your card statement. Open Therapy's fees are invoiced separately to the therapist and never appear on anything you're issued. See [Fees](/fees).

## Something's missing

An invoice appears once the session is marked as held. If a session you attended has none after a couple of days, write to [billing@opentherapy.app](mailto:billing@opentherapy.app) with the date and the therapist's name.
